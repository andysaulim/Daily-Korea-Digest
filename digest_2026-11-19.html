01Top stories
Yonhap, Trilateral

Trump, Xi, Putin issue first trilateral joint declaration, Korean Peninsula absent

Donald Trump, Xi Jinping, and Vladimir Putin met at the Kylin Villa in Shenzhen on the sidelines of the APEC summit, the first-ever US-China-Russia trilateral summit, and issued a four-point joint declaration pledging to conduct dialogue on reducing nuclear risks and preventing proliferation, reforming the United Nations, increasing production and supply of refined petroleum, and respecting state sovereignty.

The declaration made no mention of North Korea, the DPRK nuclear program, or denuclearization of the Korean Peninsula. South Korean and Chinese analysts noted the North Korea equation was absent from the communique, with Kyunghyang Shinmun reporting that the three powers' divergent nuclear calculi on the Korean Peninsula produced only a gap.

NK News, DPRK

IAEA confirms Yongbyon enrichment annex externally complete, reactor operating

IAEA Director General Rafael Grossi told the Board of Governors in Vienna that North Korea completed external construction of a new annex to the original Yongbyon uranium enrichment facility since mid-September, with the structure appearing externally complete by late October 2026. The 5 MWe reactor is continuing its seventh irradiation cycle and the light water reactor has been operating since late April 2026.

NK News reported the annex is similar in size to one built on the north side of the same Yongbyon building five years ago, and that state media in January 2025 appeared to show new centrifuge cascades filling a comparable annex interior.

Yonhap, ROK Politics

Democratic Party moves to impeach Supreme Court Chief Justice after Han Duck-soo ruling

The Supreme Court partially overturned ex-Prime Minister Han Duck-soo's 15-year prison sentence for his role in former President Yoon Suk Yeol's December 2024 martial law bid, finding that Han's proposal to convene a Cabinet meeting did not meet the threshold for insurrection intent and remanding the case to the Seoul High Court. Democratic Party leader Kim Min-seok immediately announced the party would pursue impeachment of Supreme Court Chief Justice Cho Hee-dae, calling the ruling a "historical act of treason." The People Power Party (PPP) leader Jang Dong-hyuk welcomed the ruling, calling it a court rebuke of the Lee administration's "insurrection narrative."

Reuters, ROK Economy

South Korea hits $1 trillion in annual exports, fourth country globally

South Korea's annual exports crossed $1 trillion at 2:09 p.m. on November 19, making it the fourth country globally, after Germany, the United States, and China, to reach that threshold, with AI-related semiconductors driving the surge. President Lee Jae-myung marked the milestone in a public statement, noting South Korea's exports stood at $10 billion fifty years ago.
