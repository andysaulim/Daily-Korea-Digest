Seoul proposes inter-Korean military talks on MDL as mine estimates reach 960,000

South Korea's Defense Ministry Deputy Minister for National Defense Policy Kim Hong-Cheol issued a formal statement Friday proposing military talks with North Korea to verify the exact location of the Military Demarcation Line and restore military communication lines, following land mine explosions that injured three ROK soldiers last month.

ROK lawmakers simultaneously cited intelligence estimates that Pyongyang has planted 960,000 mines in the DMZ since 2024, approximately 1,000 per day. The proposal comes as Kim Yo Jong has threatened to restart loudspeaker broadcasts within days and North Korea has ordered border troops to take resolute action against any southern threat.

Reuters / Korea Times, DPRK

US and ROK officials see little chance of Trump-Kim summit in 2026

US and South Korean officials assessed that a meeting between President Donald Trump and North Korean leader Kim Jong Un this year is unlikely, Reuters reported, citing Pyongyang's demands for Washington to abandon its denuclearization approach.

Experts Jenny Town of the Stimson Center and Ankit Panda of Carnegie, speaking on a podcast hosted by CSIS Korea Chair Victor Cha, said Pyongyang has made clear it rejects any denuclearization agenda and the real question is whether Washington is willing to shift its policy. North Korea's National Intelligence Service separately told the ROK National Assembly Intelligence Committee on Friday that social media rumors about Kim Jong Un's death are false.

Yonhap, ROK Politics

Seoul appeals court acquits Oh Se-hoon of political funds violation

A Seoul appeals court on Friday overturned a lower court ruling and acquitted Seoul Mayor Oh Se-hoon of charges related to political funds law violation stemming from the 'Myung Tae-kyun opinion poll payment' allegation.

The ruling drew sharply divergent reactions: the ruling Democratic Party called it contrary to public common sense, while the People Power Party called it inevitable justice and demanded the special prosecutor take responsibility for the prosecution.
