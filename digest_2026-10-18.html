US-Korea

USFK commander's 'southern fence line' remark on ROK sovereignty triggers political storm; MND and UNC distance themselves

USFK Commander Brunson told a Washington think tank on October 14 that 'from UNC's perspective, the ROK begins at its southern fence line' — a statement implying the DMZ falls outside ROK sovereignty. The ROK MND labeled it a 'personal view' and stated its own position on territorial sovereignty is 'firm'; UNC clarified DMZ and JSA responsibilities are 'military and administrative in nature, separate from ROK sovereignty.' The ruling Democratic Party and opposition PPP both called for Brunson to retract and apologize, while 경향신문 editorial asked whether the remark is connected to a deliberate effort to raise the bar for transfer of wartime operational control (OPCON).

Business

ROK trade surplus with US approaches $100B annual record; China deficit set to flip to surplus for first time in four years

Korea's trade surplus with the United States reached $847B through September 2026, driven by semiconductor, IT devices, and auto exports — tracking toward a first-ever annual figure above $100B and already far exceeding last year's $495B full-year surplus. Simultaneously, the deficit with China is projected to swing to surplus for the first time since 2022, driven by semiconductors, wireless devices, cosmetics, and food exports. MOTIE data note that the large US surplus increases exposure to Section 301 tariff action, while China may respond with critical mineral pressure.
