US State Dept approves potential $125M FMS sale of 103 AIM-9X Sidewinder Block II missiles to South Korea

The US State Department authorized a potential government-to-government Foreign Military Sale to South Korea of 103 AIM-9X Sidewinder Block II tactical missiles, 10 guidance units, training aids, and spare parts, estimated at $125 million, with congressional notification required before the sale can proceed. The department stated the sale supports US foreign policy and national security objectives by enhancing South Korea's air defense capabilities.

So what — Congressional review period begins now; the timing — alongside the abbreviated UFS and Trump-Kim outreach — places this sale in the context of alliance reassurance signaling.

North Korea expands Russia support to include drone operators, Ukraine says

Ukraine reported that North Korea has expanded its battlefield support for Russia to include drone operators, adding a new category of military personnel beyond the infantry and artillery troops previously documented. Kim Yo Jong separately denied Ukraine President Zelensky's claims of additional North Korean troop deployments to Russia, stating she was 'completely unaware' of any such development — a denial Pyongyang issued as a formal coordinated statement alongside its rejection of US diplomatic outreach.

So what — If confirmed, drone operator deployment represents a qualitative expansion of DPRK-Russia military-technical cooperation beyond direct combat troop deployment, relevant to the CSIS NK-Russia cooperation timeline.
