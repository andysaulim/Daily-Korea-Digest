●Top Stories
US-Korea

Lee rules out combat deployment to Middle East, holds open non-combat Hormuz role

President Lee Jae-myung told a press conference that South Korea will not dispatch troops in any manner that could lead to involvement in an overseas armed conflict, directly refusing US pressure for a military role in Middle East operations. Lee said Seoul is reviewing a possible non-combat contribution to the Strait of Hormuz and separately confirmed he has no intention of pursuing a constitutional amendment that would allow him to seek reelection. FM Cho Hyun is in Washington this week meeting Secretary Rubio, with Hormuz and the investment deal both on the agenda.

Yonhap EN · Reuters · NYT · NK News, Sep 18
DPRK

Kim Yo Jong vows 'more offensive' nuclear response to US-led Pacific drills; DPRK vice defense minister pledges closer China military cooperation at Beijing forum

Kim Yo Jong published a KCNA statement warning that North Korea may take 'more offensive' actions against US-led multinational military exercises and declared there will 'never ever' be a change in DPRK nuclear deterrent efforts. Separately, Vice Defense Minister Kim Kang Il told the 13th Beijing Xiangshan Forum that the DPRK's nuclear status is 'irreversible' and pledged closer military cooperation with China, calling US military posture 'reckless.' Kim Jong Un remains absent from state media for ten days since the Sep 8 Kang Kon commissioning.

US-Korea

Korea-US Strategic Investment Corporation mulls debut bond issuance for $350B pledge

The Korea-US Strategic Investment Corporation, established under the Special Investment Act passed March 12, 2026, is considering launching its first bond to finance the $350 billion US investment pledge. Texas power plant and nuclear energy projects are simultaneously under active discussion as components of Seoul's US investment package, per Reuters. The bond float would be the corporation's first capital markets transaction since its formation.
