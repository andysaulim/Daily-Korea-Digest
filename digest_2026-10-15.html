●Top Stories
DPRK

North Korea tests two types of submarine-launched strategic cruise missiles — first underwater launch in nearly 3 years

KCNA reported Thursday that North Korea test-fired 'two kinds of submarine-launched strategic cruise missiles' (SLCMs) from a submarine in the East Sea on Wednesday, the first underwater-launched missile tests in nearly three years. The test follows the Defense Revolution Museum opening on Oct 13 featuring the Hwasong-20 ICBM, and the Oct 12 tactical ballistic missile salvo — making this the fifth DPRK test event in October. AP released images from the test showing the launches.

China-Korea

North Korea and China officially open the New Yalu River Bridge after a decade of delay

North Korea and China opened the New Yalu River Bridge at 10 a.m. local time Thursday in a ceremony at Sinuiju, with trucks crossing in both directions per Xinhua footage — more than a decade after Beijing completed construction. The bridge is the longest NK-China border crossing, and CNN asked whether it will help Pyongyang evade UN sanctions. Nikkei reported China-North Korea cross-border traffic is expected to increase substantially.

KOSPI crashes 7.57%, circuit breaker triggered, as chipmakers rout on AI demand and inflation fears

South Korea's KOSPI fell 7.57% to close at 5,554.22 — a six-month low — as chipmaker shares led a tech selloff driven by concerns over AI demand sustainability and US inflation data. KRX activated a sell-side sidecar and then a 20-minute circuit breaker as the index dropped. Bloomberg reported Korea stocks tumbled approximately 8% as investors rushed to unwind AI exposure, with Samsung Electronics shares falling more than 9% intraday per 매일경제.
