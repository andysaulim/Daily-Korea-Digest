Zelensky: Russia preparing to receive 30,000 more DPRK troops for Ukraine war

Ukrainian President Zelensky warned in a public address on Saturday that Russia is preparing to bring in an additional 30,000 North Korean troops to fight in Ukraine — double the number previously reported — and asked partners for additional weapons and assistance to counter the threat. Zelensky stated that Russian President Putin has personally requested the additional troops. The announcement comes amid DPRK FM Choe Son-hui's recent Moscow visit in which Russia pledged to back DPRK nuclear and military development, and one day before the 73rd Korean War armistice anniversary.

So what — Directly relevant to allied governments' threat assessments ahead of the July 27 armistice anniversary and any joint ROK-US-Japan coordination on Ukraine-DPRK troop cooperation.
