●Top Stories
DPRK

North Korea fires intermediate-range ballistic missile; Kim Jong Un personally observes; Kim Yo Jong claims AI-guided low-altitude maneuvering

North Korea launched a ballistic missile from the Wonsan area at approximately 6:30 a.m. on Saturday, flying more than 700 km toward the East Sea, ROK JCS reported. Kim Yo Jong issued three sequential statements via KCNA confirming the launch was an intermediate-range strategic missile drill personally observed by 'the head of state,' and claimed the missile employs low-altitude flight trajectory changes with AI integration, asserting 'there is virtually no way to avoid this strike.' The ROK presidential National Security Office convened an emergency meeting and called for an immediate halt; MOFA held trilateral consultations with US and Japanese counterparts on the same day.

US-Korea

Trump threatens higher tariffs if Seoul rejects Alaska LNG; announces $8.4B oil recovery component; Seoul's Assembly chair pushes back

Donald Trump threatened Friday to charge South Korea 'more' if it declines to participate in the Alaska LNG project, stating he did not 'jump the gun' on announcing Korean investment despite Seoul not confirming a commitment. Trump simultaneously unveiled an $8.4 billion enhanced oil recovery component to the US-Korea deal, which the US Embassy Seoul retweeted. ROK National Assembly Finance and Economy Committee chair Cho Seung-rae stated publicly that Korean investment in the US is decided according to national interest and 'we are not in a relationship that takes unilateral US instructions.'

NK-Russia-China

Seoul's anger with Kyiv over North Korean POWs may reflect deeper worry about NK-Russia ties exposure

A Washington Post analysis reports that Seoul's demand for a formal Ukrainian apology over the handling of captured North Korean soldiers may be driven less by bilateral protocol and more by concern over the intelligence and political exposure that evidence of large-scale NK-Russia military cooperation creates. The POW issue forces Seoul to publicly manage the contradiction between maintaining the US alliance — which views NK-Russia cooperation as a strategic threat — and its own diplomatic constraints regarding Pyongyang. Both ruling Democratic Party and opposition People Power Party lawmakers separately called on Ukraine to issue a formal apology, per 뉴시스 reporting.
