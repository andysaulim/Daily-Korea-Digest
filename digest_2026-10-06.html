●Top Stories
DPRK

North Korea threatens 'immediate and powerful counteraction' if Seoul crosses 'even a millimeter' into DPRK territory; calls DMZ mine investigation a 'self-staged farce'

North Korea's Foreign Ministry issued a statement via KCNA on Tuesday calling Seoul's conclusion that DPRK-planted mines caused the DMZ blast a 'unilateral self-staged farce,' denying any responsibility and refusing to discuss its 'sovereign military activities on its own territory.' Pyongyang warned that if South Korea crosses 'even a millimeter' of DPRK territory during its announced mine-removal operation, 'the border forces of the DPRK will immediately take powerful retaliatory action.' FM Cho Hyun told lawmakers Seoul is reviewing diplomatic response options, while Defense Minister Kang Shin-chul called the mine-removal operation a 'decisive' counter-move; the Unification Ministry simultaneously signaled preference for dialogue.

Technology

Hackers using Chinese AI tool hit South Korean banks; President Lee says AI appears to have been used in attacks and orders security overhaul

A wave of cyberattacks on major South Korean financial institutions — including Shinhan, KB, Hana, and Busan banks — has been linked by investigators to hackers employing a Chinese AI tool, per WSJ reporting corroborated by NYT and FT. The Financial Services Commission pinpointed 28 IP addresses behind the attacks; police launched a criminal investigation and are weighing whether to refer the case to the new Serious Crimes Investigation Agency. President Lee Jae-myung convened an emergency cabinet session, acknowledged AI's apparent role, and ordered a full-scale review of Korea's information security architecture, while PM Han Seong-sook called for dismantling the fragmented sector-by-sector security governance structure.

DPRK

NK Pro satellite imagery reveals new coastal missile launch facility at Sangum-ri built ahead of Kim Jong Un's IRBM firing drills; Kim Yo Jong claims AI-guided evasion capability

NK Pro analysis of satellite imagery shows North Korea constructed new missile launch-related infrastructure at a coastal site in Sangum-ri, Anbyon County — a previously unreported facility — before Kim Jong Un's recent hypersonic IRBM firing drills attended by his daughter Ju Ae. NK Pro's separate analysis of the missile itself describes it as potentially North Korea's most significant strategic system reveal since the Hwasong-20 ICBM last year, with maneuverable warhead capability designed to frustrate adversary missile defenses. Kim Yo Jong publicly asserted the weapon employs AI-guided maneuvering to evade tracking — the first such public technical claim by DPRK officials.
