Business

South Korea July exports hit second-highest monthly total ever, up 62.8% on semiconductor surge

South Korea's July exports rose 62.8% year-on-year to the second-highest monthly total ever recorded, with semiconductor shipments driving the gain amid robust global demand for AI infrastructure investment, per the Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy data released August 1. Reuters attributed the beat to AI-related chip demand; the WSJ noted growth moderated from prior months but remained solid. The result — the strongest chip-led export performance in the current cycle — follows the KOSPI's record 18% single-session surge on July 31 and the won's rise to a nine-month high.

So what — The export data directly informs BOK's August monetary policy meeting calculus and the ongoing Section 301 USTR investigation into South Korean trade practices launched March 11.

US-Korea

Lee-Milei summit in Buenos Aires targets critical minerals cooperation as Lee completes Latin America tour

President Lee Jae-myung and Argentina's president held a bilateral summit in Buenos Aires on July 31, agreeing to strengthen critical minerals cooperation and signing broader bilateral cooperation agreements. The meeting follows Lee's stops in San Francisco (semiconductor investment pledges), Brazil, and Chile during the same trip, per The Diplomat's summary of the tour. Argentina holds significant lithium reserves critical to battery supply chains currently subject to IRA FEOC sourcing restrictions affecting Korean EV battery makers.

So what — Argentina lithium agreements feed directly into supply chain diversification strategies for LG Energy Solution, SK On, and Samsung SDI, which face IRA FEOC compliance deadlines for critical minerals as of December 2025.

Indonesia president offers to visit Pyongyang at Seoul's request to open inter-Korean dialogue

Indonesia's president publicly offered to visit North Korea to facilitate dialogue, stating the initiative came at the request of ROK President Lee Jae-myung, per CNA reporting. The offer represents a public third-party diplomatic intermediary proposal — a rare occurrence given Pyongyang's sustained rejection of inter-Korean engagement under the Lee administration. Kim Jong Un's July 28 statement that North Korea's nuclear capability will be updated steadily and Kim Yo Jong's rejection of ASEAN denuclearization calls remain Pyongyang's most recent formal posture signals.

So what — Directly relevant to August Ulchi Freedom Shield messaging and any ASEAN-channel diplomatic track the Lee administration may be constructing ahead of UNGA in September.
