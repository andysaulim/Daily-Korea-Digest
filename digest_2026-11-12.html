NK News, DPRK

Kim inspects east coast naval base, orders timely completion

Kim Jong Un inspected construction of a major naval base on North Korea's east coast on Nov. 11, ordering completion within the schedule and to the quality standard set by the party central, KCNA reported Nov. 12.

NK News analysis places the inspection site in Munchon, north of Wonsan, where base construction began over 12 years ago and satellite imagery showed work restarting in August 2026. Senior officials Pak Jong Chon and Jo Yong Won accompanied the inspection; NK News separately identified a second major east coast naval base project, indicating simultaneous expansion on both coasts.

Korea Times, China-Korea

FM Cho flies to Beijing Friday for Wang Yi talks on North Korea and bilateral ties

Cho Hyun will make a two-day visit to Beijing starting Friday to meet Chinese Foreign Minister Wang Yi, covering North Korea-related issues, bilateral relations and regional matters, Seoul's foreign ministry said Thursday. The visit follows Wang's August invitation during Seoul talks, where Cho urged Beijing to play a constructive role in bringing North Korea back to dialogue.

Reuters, ROK Economy

Lee says Samsung chip packaging expansion will spur $30 billion in additional investment

President Lee Jae-myung said Samsung Electronics' advanced semiconductor packaging fab expansion in Asan, South Chungcheong Province, would spur $30 billion in additional investment, Reuters reported Thursday. Lee attended the groundbreaking of three Chungcheong Mega Project anchor facilities, including a semiconductor advanced packaging fab in Asan, an advanced display production line, and an AI server high-performance substrate plant in Sejong, just four months after the project's announcement in July 2026. Six of eight anchor companies have begun active construction.
