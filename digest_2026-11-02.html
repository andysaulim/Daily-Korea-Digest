Lee submits record 820.9 trillion won budget with AI, defense, and OPCON acceleration at its core

President Lee Jae-myung presented an 820.9 trillion won ($610 billion) 2027 spending plan to the National Assembly on Monday, up 12.8% from 2026 and the largest annual budget in South Korean history.

CNBC, Technology

OpenAI CEO Altman makes unannounced Seoul visit, meets Samsung and SK Group on AI chip partnerships

Sam Altman, chief executive of OpenAI, visited South Korea over the weekend without prior announcement, meeting with approximately 50 Samsung executives in Yongin and separately having lunch with SK Group Chairman Chey Tae-won, with senior officials from SK Hynix and SK Telecom in attendance.

Samsung confirmed the Yongin meeting covered AI developments, potential business opportunities and AI's impact on corporate decision-making; SK Hynix declined to confirm the reported meeting. Altman's visit follows his October 2025 Seoul trip, when he announced Stargate partnerships with Samsung and SK Group covering high-bandwidth memory supplies and AI data center development.
