US-Korea

S. Korea, US to hold Ulchi Freedom Shield exercise Aug 17-27

The JCS and Korea-US Combined Forces Command announced Aug 10 that Ulchi Freedom Shield 26 will run Aug 17 to 27 with about 18,000 Korean troops, roughly the same as last year. Combined battalion-level-or-above field training exercises will be reduced to 14 from 17 in 2025. Allies described the drills as defensive and said they reflect evolving threats including drones, GPS disruption, and cyberattacks.

So what — The exercise window Aug 17-27 is the most likely period for DPRK rhetorical escalation or a weapons test, with Kim Jong Un unseen 13 days.

Pattern: KCNA maintained silence on the Aug 6 SRBM launch and an Aug 3 seismic event near Punggye-ri in the run-up to the exercise.

DPRK

North Korea restarts east coast naval base project for new warships, submarines

North Korea has restarted activity at an unfinished east coast naval base at Tapchon Bay slated to house new warships and submarines, according to NK Pro analysis of Planet Labs satellite imagery, after over a decade of problematic construction. Imagery suggests workers began establishing a camp at the site. The restart follows Kim Jong Un's directive to build larger and more capable warships.

So what — Adds a third active DPRK naval construction site alongside Sinpo South Shipyard and the Rajin Shipyard in Rason, all tied to Kim's fleet-expansion order.

Pattern: NK Pro reported Jul 17 that North Korea began a similar construction worker camp at the Rajin Shipyard in Rason following the same warship directive.

US-Korea

Lee approval falls to record low, drops below ruling party support

President Lee Jae Myung's approval rating fell to a new low since he took office, a Realmeter survey commissioned by Energy Economic News showed. Public support for Lee slipped below the Democratic Party's support rate for the first time in his presidency, now in its second year. Realmeter cited controversies over the real estate tax overhaul, prosecution reform, Lee's remarks linking the Korea Military Academy to a coup, plus the prolonged heat wave and economic uncertainty.

So what — The approval slide precedes the Aug 15 Liberation Day address, where Lee typically sets inter-Korean and Japan policy tone.
