01Top stories
Yonhap, DPRK

North Korea fires several missiles toward East Sea, fourth weapons launch in a week

South Korea's Joint Chiefs of Staff reported several missiles launched from the Sondok area of South Hamgyong Province toward the East Sea on Wednesday morning, with ROK-US analysis of specifications underway. Japan's Defense Ministry issued no alert, suggesting the weapons may have been shorter-range or non-ballistic types.

The launch follows approximately a dozen short-range ballistic missiles fired from Wonsan on October 29 and comes as Kim Yo Jong issued a statement calling US talks possible but not necessary, pairing diplomatic messaging with live fire on US midterm election day.

Yonhap / Korea Times, ROK Politics

Seoul High Court upholds Yoon's suspended prison term in election law case; PPP faces 39.7B won liability

The Seoul High Court confirmed a lower court's sentence of 18 months in prison, suspended for three years, against former President Yoon Suk Yeol for making false statements during the March 2022 presidential campaign.

If the ruling is finalized on further appeal, it would nullify his election and require the People Power Party to return 39.7 billion won in campaign reimbursements under election law. The PPP announced it would fight the ruling at the Supreme Court, while the Democratic Party-led legislature separately advanced the so-called fabricated-prosecution special counsel bill over PPP opposition, with the PPP threatening a filibuster.

NK News / Korea Herald / Reuters, DPRK

Kim inspects Nampho Shipyard, reveals new warship class larger than destroyers under construction

KCNA reported that Kim Jong Un inspected the Nampho Shipyard on Sunday and was shown a new warship under construction that he described as having a "huge and imposing appearance, which bears no comparison with the destroyer built last year," per NK News analysis of KCNA.

NK News analysis concluded the new vessel represents a third, larger class of warship, and that work on a third destroyer at the same facility appears to have been abandoned. Kim ordered rapid naval buildup and major infrastructure upgrades at the shipyard.
