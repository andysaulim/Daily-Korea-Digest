DMZ landmine explosion injures three ROK officers, one loses foot; origin under investigation

Three officers of the ROK Army 25th Division were injured — two seriously, one losing a foot — in a suspected landmine explosion south of the MDL during a search-route operation in the western DMZ on Monday morning. The JCS scrambled emergency helicopters into the DMZ and coordinated with the UN Command; two officers were airlifted to the Armed Forces Capital Hospital. The JCS confirmed on Sep 14 it had assessed that North Korea may have laid mines south of the MDL, and the explosion comes two days after the DPRK's dual short-range ballistic missile launches.

North Korea fires two ballistic missiles within three hours, dials pressure ahead of Trump-Xi summit

North Korea launched two short-range ballistic missiles off its east coast on Sunday in a sequence spanning approximately three hours, Japan's Defense Ministry assessed both fell outside its exclusive economic zone. The launches — the second SRBM sequence in days — are being read as a deliberate message timed to the approaching Trump-Xi summit and DPRK Vice FM Kim Son-gyong's attendance at UNGA. KCNA remained silent on the launches through Monday morning, consistent with DPRK practice of withholding claims on shorter-range tests.
