Nine of ten isolated South Koreans moved to safety after Nepal floods; nine remain missing

Nine of ten South Koreans isolated by catastrophic Nepal flooding were moved to safety by Thursday evening, the foreign ministry reported, while nine other Korean nationals — three from Korea South-East Power Co. and six from Doosan Enerbility — remained unaccounted for at a hydroelectric plant construction site in the Rasuwa region. President Lee convened an unscheduled emergency meeting at MOFA headquarters and ordered all diplomatic channels activated; a rapid-response team was dispatched, and Doosan Group announced $5 million in aid to flood-hit Nepal.

So what — Foreign Minister Cho Hyun announced a $1 million ROK humanitarian aid package through international organizations and pledged further support including a possible emergency relief team dispatch.
