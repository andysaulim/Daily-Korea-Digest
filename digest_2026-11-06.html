Bloomberg / Reuters, ROK Economy

Korea's $1.7 trillion market rout burns day traders as AI selloff follows Democratic midterm sweep

The KOSPI fell 6% on November 6, with Bloomberg reporting a $1.7 trillion rout in Korean markets as the Democratic Party's US midterm victory triggered fears of tighter AI and data-center regulation. Korean chip stocks, Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix, sank roughly 10% in pre-market trading, per a separate Bloomberg report, dragging semiconductor, battery, auto, and shipbuilding shares lower.

The Democratic Party-government emergency meeting attributed the selloff primarily to investor uncertainty over Democratic AI regulatory intentions, while financial authorities suspended the brokerage margin call ratio requirement for three months to prevent forced liquidations.

NK News, DPRK

North Korea expands warship construction project to third shipyard

The expansion to a third facility, following Nampho and at least one other previously identified site, accelerates a program that includes vessels described as cruiser-class and potentially exceeding 10,000 tons, per earlier reporting. North Korea remains silent to its domestic audience on the weapons tests and naval announcements, per NK News.

Yonhap, ROK Politics

Lee nominates defense procurement chief Lee Yong-cheol as justice minister

The nominee pledged to complete prosecution reform and establish the new Serious Crimes Investigation Agency.
