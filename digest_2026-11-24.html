Russian warplanes approach Ulleung Island, triggering air raid alert and fighter scramble

About five Russian warplanes entered the KADIZ over the East Sea early Tuesday and approached Ulleung Island close enough that the Joint Chiefs of Staff pre-emptively issued an air raid alert and scrambled fighter jets; no airspace violation was confirmed.

The interior ministry's emergency alert incorrectly stated an airspace violation, and North Gyeongsang Province's cancellation notice mistakenly described the aircraft as suspected North Korean planes. The defense ministry summoned Russian military attaché Nikolai Marchenko to lodge a formal protest; SBS reported this was the third KADIZ intrusion by Russian aircraft in November alone.

NK News / Nov 24 / Reuters / AP / Nikkei Asia / Washington Post / Arirang TV, DPRK

Kim Yo Jong threatens to force closure of Switzerland's newly opened Pyongyang mission over children's camp sanctions

Kim Yo Jong issued a KCNA statement Tuesday saying she would urge North Korea to consider 'withdrawing' the Swiss diplomatic office in Pyongyang, opened only weeks earlier, after Bern sanctioned a North Korean children's camp that had hosted Russian students.

She called Switzerland's move 'an open hostile act' and 'a serious infringement upon the sovereignty of the DPRK.' The threat follows Pyongyang's earlier warning to the United Kingdom over separate sanctions, suggesting a pattern of using newly opened diplomatic channels as leverage.

Korea Herald / Korea Times / Yonhap, Technology

KOSPI surges 6.32% to reclaim 5,000 as Jensen Huang returns to Nvidia

The Korea Composite Stock Price Index rose 297.49 points, or 6.32 percent, to close at 5,006.03, reclaiming the 5,000-point mark for the first time in three sessions, as Samsung Electronics and SK hynix rallied on Nvidia's unanimous board reinstatement of Jensen Huang as president and CEO.

Nvidia's board said it had consulted senior US government officials before concluding Huang's reinstatement posed no national security risk; Huang had stepped down November 12 after reports of an extramarital relationship with a woman suspected of links to Chinese intelligence. Nvidia has pledged to supply 260,000 graphics processing units to South Korea and Samsung and SK hynix supply high-bandwidth memory for Nvidia's AI processors.

NK News, DPRK

US DOJ seeks civil forfeiture of $1.17B in cryptocurrency stolen by North Korean hackers from Binance

The US Department of Justice filed a civil forfeiture complaint in the US District Court for the District of Columbia on Monday seeking to recover $1.17 billion in USD Coin frozen after North Korean cyber actors stole it from Binance; $823 million was held in a single virtual wallet address. The complaint targets funds frozen following an October theft attributed to DPRK-linked actors.
