Zelensky warns Russia preparing to deploy additional 10,000 North Korean troops; Moscow helped Pyongyang build attack drone factories

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky said Monday that North Korea is preparing to send approximately 10,000 more soldiers to Russia, adding to a current contingent of over 8,000 already deployed. Zelensky separately asserted that Russia has helped Pyongyang establish attack drone manufacturing capabilities in exchange for the military assistance. Concurrently, Russian President Putin ordered an army expansion, underscoring the scale of the bilateral military buildup.

NK News · Reuters · CNBC, Sep 29
DPRK

ROK-UNC joint probe finds North Korean resin mine at DMZ blast site; Defense Minister calls it armistice violation, links mines to border reinforcement

A 30-person joint ROK military and UN Command investigation team on Tuesday found a North Korean resin anti-personnel mine near the site of the Sep 21 DMZ explosion that seriously injured three soldiers during a route-clearing operation. Defense Minister Kang Shin-chul said the mine was buried and linked to Pyongyang's border reinforcement work, calling the blast a 'clear' violation of the armistice agreement and vowing 'corresponding measures.' The mine found today was assessed as different from the mine that caused the actual blast, with further investigation ongoing before a final determination.

NK News · WSJ · Reuters · WaPo · Bloomberg, Sep 29
NK-Russia-China

Seoul demands Ukraine apology after Zelensky reveals secret North Korean POW transfer; MOFA cites prior non-disclosure agreement

South Korea summoned Ukraine's charge d'affaires and demanded an apology after President Zelensky publicly disclosed that North Korean prisoners of war had been transferred to South Korea — a move Seoul says violated a prior non-disclosure understanding. The Foreign Ministry stated that a 'clear understanding' existed between Seoul and Kyiv, and the revelation created a diplomatic rupture that generated international attention on the operational details of North Korean troop deployment in Ukraine. The episode, reported by WSJ as a secretive handover now become controversial, is the first confirmed public acknowledgment that North Korean combatants captured in Ukraine have been physically transferred to South Korean custody.

WSJ · AP · WaPo · Reuters, Sep 29
US-Korea

ROK cabinet passes nuclear-powered submarine special bill; North Korea condemns US support for ROK sub ambitions at UN General Assembly

The South Korean government on Tuesday passed a special bill establishing the legal framework for introducing and operating nuclear-powered submarines. North Korea's Vice Foreign Minister Kim Son Gyong, speaking at the UN General Assembly in New York, condemned U.S. support for the ROK submarine program, saying Washington had 'approved and is encouraging Seoul's ambition to possess' nuclear-powered submarines and accused the US of worsening regional instability. The cabinet bill passage and simultaneous DPRK reaction at UNGA mark a coordinated escalation in the ROK nuclear submarine issue.
