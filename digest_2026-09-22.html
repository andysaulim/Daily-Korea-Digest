●Top Stories
DPRK

KCNA confirms Kim Jong Un oversaw test of new hypersonic missile system; NK Pro identifies concealed launcher factory

KCNA reported Tuesday that Kim Jong Un oversaw a test of a 'new-type weapon' system on Sunday — assessed by NK News analysis as a short-range missile with a hypersonic warhead — quoting Kim that the weapon would give the enemy an 'incurable headache.' NK Pro simultaneously revealed that a factory Kim previously claimed was mass-producing cargo trucks is manufacturing a new modular launch vehicle for short-range nuclear missiles, with a second launcher factory also identified in state media footage from Kim's Sep 19 munitions inspection. The disclosure occurred four days before DPRK Vice FM Kim Son-gyong is expected to address UNGA, and follows KCNA's coordinated Sep 19-20 output pairing munitions inspection, Kim Yo Jong's nuclear irreversibility declaration, and the Sep 20 dual-SRBM launches.

NK News · FT · Reuters · NK Pro, Sep 22
US-Korea

President Lee tells AP he wants step-by-step denuclearization approach, urges US to ease North Korea sanctions in exchange for freeze

Lee Jae-myung told the Associated Press in New York that he favors a step-by-step approach to North Korean nuclear dismantlement and has called on Washington to ease sanctions against Pyongyang in exchange for a freeze on its nuclear program — a public formulation that diverges from the complete, verifiable, irreversible denuclearization goal reaffirmed by the US-ROK-Japan trilateral the same day. The NYT separately reported Lee's sanctions-easing pitch; the White House has not publicly responded. The remarks came as Lee arrived in New York for UNGA, where his address is the key diplomatic set piece of the week.

AP · WaPo · NYT (via Reuters), Sep 22
US-Korea

New Defense Minister Kang Shin-chul inaugurated, chairs all-commanders meeting after DMZ landmine injures three ROK troops

Kang Shin-chul was sworn in as South Korea's 52nd Defense Minister on Tuesday and immediately convened a full-military commanders meeting at the Joint Chiefs operations center, his first official act. Kang expressed condolences to three ROK officers injured in a suspected North Korean landmine explosion during a DMZ route clearance operation the previous day and pledged the state would bear responsibility for their care, while ordering a thorough investigation with all possibilities open. USFK Commander Gen. Paul LaCamera separately welcomed Kang at the defense ministry and posted that their combined defense commitment remains 'unshakeable.'

Yonhap · ROK MND · USFK, Sep 22
US-Korea

Seoul names Texas power plant as first confirmed project under $350B US investment pact, despite viability concerns

South Korea has selected the construction of a large-scale power plant in Texas as the first project under the government-level $350B US-Korea Strategic Investment Corporation framework, sources told Yonhap, with Reuters reporting the selection despite ongoing viability questions and unresolved US-ROK implementation negotiations. Seoul and Washington had still been working through project details ahead of a planned National Assembly briefing, per an earlier Yonhap report. 경향신문's editorial warned the government must not sacrifice commercial rationality in its rush to demonstrate pledge momentum.
