KOSPI plunges 6% as Democratic midterm sweep triggers AI selloff

South Korea's KOSPI fell 6.02% on November 6, briefly breaching 5,000 intraday, as Democratic Party victories in Texas, Ohio, and Georgia gubernatorial races sparked fears of tighter AI data-center regulation and reduced demand for semiconductors. Taiwan and Japan equity markets fell on the same session, reflecting shared exposure to AI infrastructure investment.

Kim Jong Un orders Nampho Shipyard expansion as warship construction spreads to third shipyard

Kim Jong Un inspected Nampho Shipyard and ordered strengthening of surface and underwater naval capabilities alongside modernization of the facility, with speculation mounting that a vessel under construction may exceed 10,000 tons. NK News reports North Korea has now expanded warship construction to a third shipyard, beyond Nampho and Sinpo.

Dong-A Ilbo (English), ROK Politics

Lee names Lee Yong-cheol justice minister nominee, plus tax and police chiefs

President Lee Jae-myung nominated Lee Yong-cheol, 66, head of the Defense Acquisition Program Administration and a former Roh Moo-hyun administration legal aide, as justice minister candidate, 48 days after the previous nominee withdrew.

The People Power Party immediately demanded the nominations be withdrawn, citing Lee Yong-cheol's ties to the Seongnam circle and alleged Daejangdong-related connections, while the Democratic Party rejected the claims as false.
