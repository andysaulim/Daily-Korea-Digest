●Top Stories
DPRK

DPRK rejects ROK mine investigation, warns any demining crossing its territory will draw forceful retaliation

North Korea condemned Seoul's final investigation into the Sept. 21 DMZ mine blast that injured three ROK soldiers and warned its forces would respond to any South Korean demining crossing into DPRK territory. The DPRK statement came via KCNA as the ROK Defense Ministry simultaneously announced plans to seek a formal armistice violation citation through UN Command. ROK PM Han Seong-sook separately said Seoul must keep demanding a North Korean apology, while Unification Minister Chung Dong-young described the situation as underscoring the need for 'fundamental peace.'

NK News / Yonhap / Reuters, Oct 7
Technology

Nuri rocket completes fifth launch, deploying 15 satellites including ROK's first microsatellite constellation

South Korea's homegrown Nuri rocket lifted off from Naro Space Center on Oct. 7 and successfully deployed 15 satellites into orbit at 570 km, including five constellation satellites released sequentially — the country's first microsatellite constellation. President Lee called the achievement proof of South Korea's expanded launch capacity and urged further advances in satellite technology. Private sector participation in the mission was highlighted as a milestone toward a 'New Space' civilian-led era.

Yonhap English, Oct 7
NK-Russia-China

Kim Jong Un vows to deepen strategic ties with Russia, backs China on Taiwan in coordinated KCNA statements

Kim Jong Un sent a letter to Vladimir Putin vowing to deepen the 'strategic' partnership and calling Russia's war in Ukraine 'sacred,' per KCNA via Reuters and the Kyiv Post. Simultaneously, North Korea issued a statement criticizing US support for Taiwan and pledging backing for Beijing's 'One China' position, framing the US as responsible for regional instability. The dual-track diplomatic messaging — to Moscow on its war and to Beijing on Taiwan — arrived ahead of the Workers' Party founding day on Oct. 10.
