KOSPI closes down 3%+ as Samsung shareholder return plan disappoints, chip sector sells off

Seoul stocks closed sharply lower Monday as Samsung's shareholder return plan fell short of market expectations, triggering a broad chip-sector selloff that drove the KOSPI down more than 3% — its worst single-day performance in recent weeks. The won simultaneously hit a near 13-month high against the dollar as dollar selling continued, with USD/KRW at 1,384.80. The dual move — equities down, won up — reflects diverging pressures on Korean financial markets as Nvidia earnings loom and investors reassess Samsung's capital allocation posture.

So what — Directly affects Samsung's standing in the $350B US-Korea investment tracker and its competitive position ahead of a likely presidential meeting on chip projects.

US-Korea

Special counsel wraps 180-day probe after indicting Yoon Suk-yeol and 57 others

Special counsel Kwon Chang-young's team concluded its 180-day martial law investigation on Monday, having indicted former President Yoon Suk-yeol and 57 others — the largest prosecutorial action stemming from the December 2024 martial law declaration. Simultaneously, President Lee Jae-myung appointed a former prosecutor as special inspector to investigate corruption involving the president's own family and close aides, accepted the justice minister's resignation, and reiterated that 'law is a universal principle that should be upheld unwaveringly.' An appellate court separately upheld an 18-month prison term for a former presidential aide over a martial law document.

So what — The parallel special counsel closure and new inspector appointment signal that accountability proceedings from the Yoon era and scrutiny of the Lee administration are running on concurrent tracks.

DPRK

North Korea and Belarus near visa-waiver agreement for ordinary citizens

North Korea and Belarus are close to signing a visa-waiver agreement for ordinary citizens, according to a report relayed by Yonhap, which would represent an expansion of DPRK's post-isolation diplomatic linkages with Belarus — a close Russian partner. The agreement would facilitate movement of North Korean nationals into the Belarusian corridor, a route with potential relevance for DPRK labor deployments into the Russia-aligned sphere. The development comes as DPRK's troop deployments to Russia remain a live issue, with Seoul confirming it sees no signs of additional troop deployments to Ukraine as of Aug 23.

So what — Adds a new logistics and labor corridor to the NK-Russia-Belarus alignment axis; relevant to the CSIS NK-Russia bilateral event timeline.
