NK-Russia-China

New Russian ambassador Andrei Podelyshev arrives in Pyongyang, vows to maximize bilateral cooperation

Andrei Podelyshev arrived in Pyongyang on Sep 26 aboard a direct Nordwind flight from Moscow with his wife, nearly 10 months after his predecessor died in post — the cause of death was never officially announced by either side. Podelyshev immediately visited the Liberation Tower to honor Soviet soldiers killed in 1945, and separately paid respects to North Korean troops killed fighting in Kursk, and toured the 'Memorial Hall of Combat Feats of Overseas Military Operations.' He told TASS his mission is to 'activate practical cooperation' and 'make maximum use of the enormous potential' of bilateral ties, attributing the relationship's current high level to Putin and Kim Jong Un personally.

US-Korea

ROK military and UNC enter DMZ five days after land-mine blast to prepare joint investigation

The Joint Chiefs of Staff and UNC Military Armistice Commission conducted a preliminary DMZ reconnaissance on Sep 26, five days after a suspected land-mine detonation wounded three soldiers — two seriously — in an area south of the Military Demarcation Line in Paju, Gyeonggi Province on Sep 21. JCS Vice Chairman Gen. Kwon Dae-won led the approximately 20-person team, which assessed route safety, surveillance, and communications without entering the blast site itself. Opposition lawmaker Han Dong-hoon held a press conference at the Defense Ministry alleging the ROK government refused UNC Commander Gen. Xavier Brunson's request for immediate access on Sep 22, delaying the formal joint investigation to Sep 27 or later.

DPRK

North Korean crypto theft in 2026 surpasses $1 billion after $352M Bitget heist

The Bitget cryptocurrency exchange hack — attributed by the exchange's CEO to North Korean cybercriminals — stole $351.6 million in digital assets from the Seychelles-based platform's internet-connected hot wallets, Bloomberg reports, pushing Pyongyang's 2026 crypto haul past $1 billion. NK Pro had separately confirmed the Bitget link to DPRK actors. North Korean cyber operations fund weapons procurement and circumvent UN sanctions; the UN Panel of Experts previously assessed DPRK cyber theft as a primary sanctions evasion mechanism.
