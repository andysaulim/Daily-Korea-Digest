Kim Jong Un calls Ukraine war a 'sacred war,' vows to expand Russia ties ahead of Russian parliamentary election

Kim Jong Un sent a personal letter to Vladimir Putin — dated Monday, responding to Putin's congratulatory telegram on the DPRK founding anniversary — declaring unwavering support for Russia in what he termed a 'sacred war' and pledging to expand bilateral ties. Reuters also reported Kim's message, citing KCNA. The letter comes as Russia heads into parliamentary elections and days after KCNA confirmed the September 12 multi-type nuclear firepower drill, placing Kim's political messaging in direct sequence with his military signaling.

DPRK

KCNA confirms September 12 launches as multi-type nuclear firepower drill — three missile types plus drones

KCNA ended two days of post-launch silence by officially characterizing the September 12 launches as a 'concentrated strike drill' demonstrating 'huge destructive power,' involving multiple ballistic missile types and drones. AP reported KCNA described the exercise as showcasing the capability to conduct nuclear counterstrikes. The drill came one day after the US-Japan-ROK trilateral Freedom Edge 26 exercise concluded (Sep 7-11), consistent with the DPRK pattern of post-allied-exercise live-fire responses.
