US State Department, Trilateral

US, ROK, Japan FMs sign Arctic-Pacific partnership pact, reaffirm DPRK denuclearization at G20

Cho Hyun, Marco Rubio, and Japanese Foreign Minister Toshimitsu Motegi met on the margins of the G20 in Atlanta on October 30 and signed a Memorandum of Cooperation for a Trilateral Arctic Pacific Partnership (TAPP) covering maritime cooperation and economic partnership in the Pacific Arctic.

The three foreign ministers reaffirmed their 'resolute commitment to the complete denuclearization' of North Korea and called for full implementation of UN Security Council resolutions. ROK MOFA readout notes the meeting was held approximately one month after a prior trilateral on the sidelines of the UN General Assembly, the fourth such trilateral in four months.

Dong-A Ilbo, DPRK

North Korea exhibits drones targeting mock ROK guard post and US tank at 'Unmanned Aviation Technology Development-2026'

Kim Jong Un attended the October 29 'Unmanned Aviation Technology Development-2026' exhibition, where North Korea displayed at least 15 drone types across strategic, operational, and tactical categories and conducted live demonstrations, including a Harop-resembling loitering munition destroying a mock South Korean border guard post on an island and an X-shaped drone striking mock-ups of a South Korean K9 howitzer and a US M1 Abrams tank.

A Bulsae-4 air-to-ground missile fired from a Satbyeol-9 unmanned attack aircraft struck a mock counter-battery radar. The exhibition followed Pyongyang's rejection of Seoul's proposal for military talks and its October 29 launch of approximately 10 short-range ballistic missiles toward the sea.

The Hill / The Hill International, DPRK

Trump says US investigating Binance hack; blockchain firms tie $6.3B theft to North Korean group TraderTraitor

President Donald Trump said in the Oval Office that the US is investigating whether North Korea is responsible for the $6.3 billion hack of Binance, the largest cryptocurrency theft on record. Blockchain intelligence firms TRM Labs and Chainalysis both attributed the theft to TraderTraitor, a North Korean hacking unit with a history of targeting digital asset platforms.

Dong-A Ilbo, US-Korea

Seoul signals OPCON transfer could advance in early 2027, eyes December SCM for timeline

Wi Sung-lac, director of national security at the Presidential Office, said South Korea and the US completed a Full Operational Capability (FOC) capability assessment during this year's Ulchi Freedom Shield exercise, positioning the allies to set a target year for wartime operational control (OPCON) transfer.

Seoul hopes to settle the timeline at a ROK-US defense ministers' meeting in December followed by the Security Consultative Meeting (SCM) early next year; a senior Presidential Office official said Washington is not opposed to the transfer itself. Wi also said Seoul demanded an apology and concrete accountability from North Korea over the September DMZ land mine explosion.
