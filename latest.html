NIS monitors signs of imminent Kim Jong Un visit to China

South Korea's National Intelligence Service said Saturday it is 'monitoring various developments' amid speculation that Kim Jong Un could make another visit to China.

Speculation centers on Chinese President Xi Jinping's briefing to President Lee Jae-myung about North Korea-China relations at their APEC Shenzhen summit last week; the presidential office said there was 'no mention to the effect reported by the media' but confirmed it is closely monitoring North Korea-China relations. Kim made an unannounced one-day visit to Dandong on October 24, where he met Wang Huning, China's top political adviser.

Yonhap News Agency / KCNA, NK-Russia-China, DPRK state media

North Korea and Russia hold 13th economic cooperation committee meeting, agree to boost joint projects

North Korea and Russia convened the 13th Meeting of their Intergovernmental Committee for Cooperation in Trade, Economy, Science and Technology in Pyongyang, agreeing to activate bilateral cooperation projects. KCNA reported the meeting under the headline 'Respected Comrade Kim Jong Un Sends Congratulatory Message to President of Mauritania' in the same news cycle, with the economic committee meeting listed separately.

The meeting follows Russia's Nov. 24 agreement to pay North Korea in fuel after a currency dispute, the latest in a series of bilateral economic transactions tracked since late 2022.

Korea Herald / Yonhap, Technology

Lee breaks ground on GS Group's 2.4 GW AI data center campus in Gangwon, calling AI race a 'silent war'

President Lee Jae-myung attended the groundbreaking of GS Group's AI data center campus in Donghae, Gangwon Province, the first of the government's three major megaprojects to begin construction.

GS Group Chairman Huh Tae-soo attended alongside Science and ICT Minister Bae Kyung-hoon; the facility is projected at up to 120 trillion won ($89.79 billion) in total value including GPU costs, with an initial 1.2 GW phase targeting completion and operation in 2029. Lee described the global AI technology race as 'a silent war at the national level' and pledged to 'mobilize all capabilities' to make South Korea a standard-setting country.
