North Korea tests new high-speed torpedo, announces early-2027 Navy deployment

North Korea's Academy of Defense Sciences, Missile Administration, and Torpedo Weapon Research Bureau jointly tested a "new-type high-speed torpedo weapon system" on Monday, with KCNA announcing the weapon will be equipped by the Navy from the beginning of 2027.

The test is the second public test of the weapon in a month; the first was disclosed after an October 15 test. KCNA released no photographs and fewer technical details than the initial disclosure.

Korea Herald / Yonhap, US-Korea

Seoul Cabinet approves Cheonghae unit expansion toward Hormuz; Assembly consent next

The motion, submitted to the National Assembly, where the ruling Democratic Party holds a majority, also adds "support for the restoration of freedom of navigation" to the unit's mandate. Iran's foreign ministry had urged Seoul on Monday to refrain from deployment to the Hormuz area; Seoul reiterated its "non-involvement, non-intervention" principle.

Yonhap / Korea Herald, US-Korea

Lee calls for bipartisan US congressional outreach after Democratic midterm sweep

President Lee Jae-myung told a Cabinet meeting that South Korea must maintain close cooperation with the US administration while "establishing a bipartisan foundation for cooperation in Congress" following the Democratic Party's seizure of both chambers in last week's midterm elections.

Lee instructed officials to prepare scenario-based responses to external uncertainties and to minimize negative effects on the domestic economy. Pro-Korea Democratic lawmakers are emerging as frontrunners to chair key House and Senate foreign affairs and security committees, according to Paijinancial News.

NK News, DPRK

Kim Yo Jong rejects UN Secretary-General's inter-Korean dialogue call, threatens consequences

Kim Yo Jong accused UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres of taking a "prejudiced and irresponsible attitude" and "echoing the ROK's unilateral and provocative sophism" after Guterres called for renewed inter-Korean dialogue following a Demilitarized Zone landmine incident.

In her statement, Kim warned that if "reckless military provocations of the ROK gangsters lead to an undesirable incident in the southern border area," the UN Secretary-General would bear responsibility. The statement was also carried by Reuters.
