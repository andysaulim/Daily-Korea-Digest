01Top stories
NK News / Korea Herald / Korea Times, DPRK

Kim attends drone expo, North Korea fires ~10 ballistic missiles from Wonsan

Kim Jong Un attended the 'Unmanned Aeronautical Technology Development-2026' exhibition on Thursday, calling for 'absolute superiority' in drone technology and observing live-fire demonstrations of suicide attack drones, tactical cruise missiles, and a 600mm multiple rocket launcher dubbed the 'Sniper', publicly demonstrated in a firing exercise for the first time, per KCNA.

Seoul's Joint Chiefs of Staff separately detected around 10 short-range ballistic missiles fired from the Wonsan area toward the East Sea that same day, though the US assessed only 2 ballistic missile launches, with the discrepancy unresolved. South Korea's National Security Office said the launches violated UN Security Council resolutions; trilateral director-general consultations among Seoul, Washington, and Tokyo were held by phone on October 30.

NK News / Hankyoreh, US-Korea

NIS confirms US restricted intelligence sharing after Seoul disclosed North Korean nuclear site

South Korea's National Intelligence Service (NIS) Director Lee Jong-seok confirmed to the National Assembly Intelligence Committee on October 29 that the United States began restricting intelligence sharing after Unification Minister Chung Dong-young publicly disclosed information about a suspected North Korean nuclear facility earlier this year.

The NIS separately told the committee that US visa delays affecting NIS personnel assigned to the US Embassy have been resolved and that those personnel are now working normally, characterizing the delays as a global US entry policy tightening rather than a bilateral dispute. The intelligence-sharing restriction adds a new dimension to the ongoing tension over wartime operational control (OPCON) transfer talks, which US and ROK defense chiefs agreed on October 28 to delay to early next year.

Korea JoongAng Daily / UPI / Chosun Ilbo, ROK Economy

Korea Hydro and Nuclear Power ends contractor talks, putting $18.7B Czech nuclear plant at risk

Korea Hydro and Nuclear Power (KHNP) terminated negotiations with Daewoo Engineering and Construction and Doosan Enerbility over their subcontracting role in the $18.7 billion Czech Dukovany nuclear plant project, citing cost disputes after talks collapsed.

KHNP vowed no delay to the overall project timeline but has not named replacement contractors. The breakdown comes as Japan's IHI is actively competing for overseas nuclear demand in the same European market.

Korea.net / Reuters, ROK Politics

Lee and Merz launch 'enhanced strategic partnership,' sign double-tax treaty amendment

President Lee Jae-myung and German Chancellor Friedrich Merz held a bilateral summit at Cheong Wa Dae on October 29, announcing an 'enhanced strategic partnership' covering advanced technology, defense, and critical minerals.

The two sides signed a Korea-Germany double taxation treaty amendment after nine years of negotiations and agreed to pursue a general security of military information agreement. Lee separately urged the European Union to grant Korean firms equal treatment under the proposed EU Industrial Accelerator Act, positioning Seoul as a key EU trading partner.
