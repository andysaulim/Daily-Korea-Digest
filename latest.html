NK-Russia-China

Russia and North Korea open first road bridge across their shared Tumen River border

Russia and North Korea formally opened the first road bridge across their shared border at a ceremony attended by Russian PM Mikhail Mishustin; the bridge over the Tumen River is named after Soviet officer Yakov Novichenko. The bridge constitutes the first permanent road crossing between the two countries, supplementing the existing rail link used since September 2023 for arms shipments. NK News analysis notes the naming and ceremony design reflect deliberate symbolic investment in the bilateral relationship alongside the geopolitical infrastructure.
