WSJ / Yonhap / CNA, DPRK

Kim Jong Un spurned Trump's meeting offer; Trump administration had explored Korean War end declaration to secure a summit

Kim Jong Un declined to meet President Donald Trump during Trump's Asia trip, with Yonhap and the WSJ reporting the Trump administration actively pursued a summit and had explored various inducements, including a Korean War end-of-war declaration, with North Korea ultimately presenting conditions Washington found unacceptable.

Trump said at a press conference that the meeting 'didn't work out' but vowed to 'have that meeting' in the future; North Korea's state media made no reference to the offer. Kim instead conducted a publicized inspection of educational factory sites, the dominant domestic image of the day.

Yonhap, ROK Politics

Education Minister Choi Kyo-jin resigns over AI cheating chaos ahead of Suneung; Lee accepts within one hour

Choi Kyo-jin resigned as education minister on November 22, one hour after announcing his intention to step down over last-minute anti-AI-cheating rules that caused widespread confusion ahead of the College Scholastic Ability Test (Suneung) held November 19.

President Lee Jae-myung formally approved the dismissal and instructed Prime Minister Han Seong-sook to take full responsibility for follow-up measures; Lee posted on X that 'the fairness of exams is a principle that cannot be compromised.' The People Power Party leader Jang Dong-hyuk said the minister's resignation was insufficient and demanded the government explain the root cause of the failure.

ArmsControlWonk, DPRK

New satellite imagery confirms additional expansion at Yongbyon uranium enrichment plant

New commercial satellite photos published by the WSJ and analyzed by Jeffrey Lewis at ArmsControlWonk show a small but concrete addition to North Korea's original uranium enrichment plant (UEP) at Yongbyon, separate from the previously reported new centrifuge hall expansion.

The imagery corroborates the sustained upward trend in enrichment-related construction at the complex that analysts have tracked through 2026. North Korea's nuclear program is expanding as Trump, Xi Jinping, and Vladimir Putin held their first trilateral summit in Shenzhen, at which Trump said Korean Peninsula dangers were discussed.

Hankyoreh, ROK Economy

KOSPI plunges 5.21% on November 20; government convenes emergency 'F4' meeting and prepares market stabilization fund

The KOSPI closed November 20 down 5.21% at 4,869.96, breaching the 5,000 level, after a global AI-sector selloff triggered by NVIDIA's stock rout, with sell-side circuit breakers (saiducar) activated.

Deputy Prime Minister Lee Hyung-il convened an emergency inter-agency meeting that afternoon with Financial Services Commission Chairman Lee Eok-won, Financial Supervisory Service Director Lee Chan-jin, and Bank of Korea Governor Shin Hyun-song to prepare activation of the market stabilization fund (jeunganpendo) and ease daily share buyback limits for three months effective November 24. Goldman Sachs' panic index reached 9 out of 10, per Bloomberg, with foreign net selling of Korean equities totaling 45 trillion won over September-October.
