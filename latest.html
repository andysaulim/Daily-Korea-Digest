Seoul Ambassador Confirms Intelligence Cooperation Is 'One of the Most Difficult Issues' in US-ROK Ties

South Korea's ambassador Kang Kyung-wha told National Assembly lawmakers that intelligence cooperation with the US has become one of the most difficult issues in bilateral relations, confirming Washington raised concerns over the Unification Minister's public remarks about a suspected DPRK uranium enrichment facility.
