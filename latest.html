Hankyoreh, China-Korea

FM Cho, Wang Yi meet in Beijing ahead of Lee-Xi APEC summit

Cho Hyun met Wang Yi at Beijing's Diaoyutai State Guesthouse on November 13, with Cho calling the Korean Peninsula issue 'South Korea's vital interest' and Wang assessing that the bilateral relationship has 'returned to the normal track of improvement and development.' Both sides cited a projected 1,000-fold increase in people-to-people exchanges and more than 40% trade growth in 2026.

NK News / Bloomberg, DPRK

DOJ obtains court authorization to seize $1.2B in DPRK-linked Binance funds

The US Department of Justice announced coordinated actions to disrupt North Korea's cryptocurrency theft operations, obtaining court authorization to seize over $1.2 billion stolen from Binance.

The actions target two operations attributed to DPRK state-sponsored actors: a social engineering campaign dubbed 'FaceStealer' and a separate exchange hack. Bloomberg separately reported the broader context of a $2.7 billion freeze linked to the same DPRK Binance hacking operation.

NK News / Yonhap, DPRK

North Korea changes flag aspect ratio for first time since 1948

North Korea's official website Naenara began displaying its national flag with a new aspect ratio of 1:1.65, replacing the 1:2 ratio that had been in place since the country's establishment in 1948. The two thin white stripes also appear thicker than before.

The change, embedded in the revised constitution, is the latest in a series of moves by Kim Jong Un to build a distinct national identity separate from the founding era of Kim Il Sung.

Reuters / Korea Times, Technology

South Korea confirms Nvidia will keep GPU supply pledges after Jensen Huang's resignation

South Korea's science ministry confirmed that Nvidia has reaffirmed its commitment to supply 260,000 graphics processing units (GPUs) to South Korea for AI factory buildouts despite the resignation of CEO Jensen Huang following affair allegations.

The ministry held a meeting with Nvidia on pending issues and confirmed cooperation plans with Samsung Electronics, SK Group, Hyundai Motor Group, and Naver Cloud remain on track. The science minister posted separately on Facebook noting GPU supply and Korean partnership programs would proceed as company-level commitments.
