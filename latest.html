Top stories
Yonhap English / Yonhap North Korea KO / NK News Oct 21, DPRK

Kim Yo Jong orders loudspeakers along full border by October month-end

Kim Yo Jong, North Korean leader Kim Jong Un's sister, announced October 21 that anti-South Korea psychological warfare loudspeakers will be activated along the entire inter-Korean border line by the end of October.

The announcement came one day after she and Marshal Pak Jong Chon inspected southern border fortification sites and ordered troops to 'counterattack immediately' if South Korean forces threaten their safety. The ROK MND had already vowed 'decisive retaliation' against North Korean provocations following Kim Yo Jong's October 19 loudspeaker threat.

Reuters / Yonhap English, ROK Economy

South Korea October 1-20 exports surge 105% on record chip sales

South Korea's exports for the first 20 days of October surged 105% year-on-year, driven by record semiconductor shipments, per customs data reported by Reuters.

The won extended gains to a fresh two-plus-year high against the US dollar on the strength of the trade data. The BOK rate decision is due this week, with 93% of bond experts surveyed forecasting a rate freeze.

AP News / Korea JoongAng Daily / Yonhap English, US-Korea

Seoul withdraws more embassy staff from Ukraine as North Korean POW row deepens

South Korea pulled additional diplomatic staff from its embassy in Kyiv following the earlier recall of its ambassador, deepening a rift over Seoul's disclosure of North Korean prisoners of war captured by Ukraine.

The main opposition People Power Party labeled the move an 'emotional response,' while European partners voiced unease over the rupture with a North Atlantic Treaty Organization ally. The dispute centers on Seoul's handling of the timing and manner of the North Korean POW repatriation announcement.
