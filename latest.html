Top Stories
DPRK

DPRK commissions destroyer Kang Kon, Kim Jong Un declares naval nuclear armament

North Korea commissioned the destroyer Kang Kon at Wonsan — the same warship that capsized during its 2025 launch — with Kim Jong Un declaring the vessel part of the DPRK's nuclear response system and calling for the navy to be nuclear-armed. Kim appeared alongside his daughter Ju Ae at the ceremony; DPRK defense minister Kim Song Gi separately warned of 'more powerful and resolute action' against US-ROK-Japan Freedom Edge drills running September 7-11. The commissioning marks DPRK's second nuclear-capable destroyer and the first explicit naval nuclear armament declaration.

NK-Russia-China

Russia-North Korea open first road bridge over Tumen River — PM Mishustin presides

Russia and North Korea formally opened the first road bridge across their shared border, over the Tumen River, in a ceremony attended by Russian Prime Minister Mikhail Mishustin; the bridge is named after Soviet officer Yakov Novichenko. NK News analysis notes the bridge combines strategic symbolism — deepening the bilateral axis — with practical geopolitical utility, providing the first permanent road route linking the two countries. KCNA confirmed the opening; Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin also sent congratulatory messages to Kim Jong Un on North Korea's 78th founding anniversary pledging closer 'strategic' ties.

US-Korea

Seoul dispatches Hormuz assessment team; Lee-Macron agree on joint freedom-of-navigation coordination

South Korea's Ministry of National Defense dispatched an investigation team to assess conditions in the Strait of Hormuz, while the Blue House clarified that Seoul is examining 'contribution methods' and not considering combat deployment. At the Élysée Palace summit, President Lee and French President Macron agreed to coordinate closely on Hormuz freedom of navigation — Macron describing the multinational mission as 'peaceful' and pledging cooperation 'as soon as consensus is obtained.' A US official separately stated the strait is 'completely open, controlled by the US.'

US-Korea

Lee-Macron Paris summit: military pacts signed, Macron condemns DPRK support for Russia

Presidents Lee Jae-myung and Emmanuel Macron signed multiple agreements in Paris covering military information sharing, logistics, and defense industry cooperation; Lee received France's Legion of Honor grand cross. Macron condemned North Korea's support for Russia's war in Ukraine and expressed support for Korean Peninsula peace, while Lee described the two leaders as genuine friends — using the Korean term 'kkopaeng.' Senior business figures including Lee Jae-yong (Samsung), Koo Ja-eun (LG), and Cho Hyun-joon (Hyosung) attended the accompanying business roundtable.
