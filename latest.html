Kim Jong Un makes unannounced visit to Dandong via new Yalu bridge, met Chinese Premier

Kim Jong Un crossed into China's Dandong via the newly opened New Yalu River Bridge on October 24, the ROK Unification Ministry confirmed, marking his first visit to China in over a year. The Presidential Office stated it was monitoring the situation closely, noting no official announcement from either side.

Democratic Party National Assembly Intelligence Committee member Yun Geon-young told SBS that Kim likely met with Chinese Premier Li Qiang and returned to North Korea the same day, with the visit timed to the October 25 anniversary of China's entry into the Korean War.

NK News / Reuters / Korea JoongAng Daily / Yonhap / Oct 24, DPRK

Kim Jong Un calls for 'military revolution' at KPA company commanders conference

Kim Jong Un oversaw the Third Meeting of Company Leaders of the Korean People's Army (KPA) in Pyongyang on October 22-23, calling for perfecting low-level leadership, improving training systems, and building the world's strongest armed forces, per the Korean Central News Agency (KCNA).

Kim delivered a speech framing the drive as a "military revolution" targeting unit-level command. The conference preceded his departure for Dandong, combining domestic military signaling with a diplomatic move toward Beijing.

Washington Post / Oct 24, DPRK

Seoul proposes military talks with Pyongyang to address border tensions; North Korea silent

South Korea's Ministry of National Defense proposed military-to-military talks with North Korea to resolve disputes over the Military Demarcation Line (MDL) and ease rising border tensions, per The Washington Post.

Yonhap reported North Korea had not responded by the following day. The proposal follows North Korea's laying of 960,000 mines near the Demilitarized Zone since 2024, per Dong-A Ilbo, and Kim Yo Jong's ongoing border fortification inspections and loudspeaker warnings.
