NK News / Korea Times / Yonhap / Reuters / WSJ / AP / Oct 25, NK-Russia-China

Kim returns from Dandong after first China visit since September 2025, touts 'blood alliance'

Kim Jong Un crossed the New Yalu River Bridge on October 24 to visit Dandong, where he toured the Memorial Hall to the Chinese People's Volunteers and met Wang Huning, the fourth-highest-ranking Chinese official, and separately held talks with Chinese Foreign Minister Wang Yi, according to KCNA and Yonhap.

Kim said North Korea is 'inspired' by China's success and vowed to advance the relationship 'forged in blood,' framing the visit around the 76th anniversary of China's entry into the Korean War. The visit was a day-trip with no advance notice and no meeting with Xi Jinping, a departure from prior state-level summits; his delegation included party presidium member Jo Yong-won, Foreign Minister Choe Son-hui, and Kim Yo-jong.

Yonhap / Maeil Business, ROK Economy

Lee says South Korea's Q2 current account surplus surpassed Germany and Japan combined

President Lee Jae-myung posted Sunday that South Korea's second-quarter current account surplus exceeded the combined surpluses of Germany and Japan, describing the result as 'a great wave of opportunity' driven by semiconductor exports.

Lee said South Korea is now a leading economy rather than a follower of advanced nations, and called for the surplus to translate into jobs and income gains. Maeil Business separately reported that South Korea's current account surplus reached second place globally, while September exports hit a record $121.1 billion, led by semiconductors.

Korea Times / Chosun Ilbo, ROK Politics

Lee narrows Justice Ministry shortlist to two prosecutor-turned-ruling-party lawmakers

A presidential office source told Yonhap that President Lee Jae-myung is reviewing Democratic Party lawmakers Park Kyoon-taek and Yang Bu-nam as candidates to lead the Ministry of Justice, with a formal nomination expected soon.

Lee's previous nominee, Rep. Kim Seung-won, withdrew last month after lobbying allegations arose during his parliamentary confirmation hearing. Opposition People Power Party figures, including Na Kyung-won, accused Lee of seeking a justice minister who would move to drop charges against him.
