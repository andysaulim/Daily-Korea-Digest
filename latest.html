North Korea tests tactical ballistic missile cluster warhead, vows four types in service by year-end

North Korea's Missile Administration conducted an October 31 live-fire test of the 'Standard Type 2' cluster-bomb warhead designed for tactical ballistic missiles, claiming it can devastate 12 to 13 hectares, equivalent to 18 soccer fields, with a single detonation. KCNA published imagery showing the warhead dispersing submunitions over mock-ups of South Korean military facilities including arched-roof structures resembling aircraft hangars and ammunition depots.

The Missile Administration director stated all four categories of cluster warheads will be fielded by military units before end-2026; South Korea's Joint Chiefs confirmed a ballistic missile launch from North Pyongan Province toward the Yellow Sea on October 31, identifying the likely projectile as the KN-24.

Reuters / AP / Bloomberg, DPRK

FBI formally attributes $6.3B Binance cryptocurrency theft to North Korea's TraderTraitor unit

The Federal Bureau of Investigation formally attributed the record-breaking theft of approximately $6.3 billion from Binance to TraderTraitor, a North Korean state-linked hacking unit, according to blockchain intelligence firms TRM Labs and Chainalysis and confirmed by Reuters and AP.

President Donald Trump told reporters that if North Korea's involvement is confirmed, he would seek direct talks with Pyongyang, the first time he has linked the crypto heist to a diplomatic opening. The UK government separately warned that North Korean information technology workers posing as legitimate employees have targeted thousands of British banks and companies.

Bloomberg / Reuters / WSJ / Forbes Middle East, ROK Economy

South Korea October exports hit record $115.3B, chip boom puts $1 trillion annual target in reach

South Korea's October exports reached approximately $115.3 billion, nearly doubling year-on-year on surging AI-driven semiconductor demand, according to Korea Customs Service data reported by Reuters, Bloomberg, and WSJ.

The monthly figure is the highest on record, cutting the gap to the annual $1 trillion target to approximately $70 billion with two months remaining. Semiconductor shipments led the surge, with the WSJ reporting that chip-led exports are accelerating as global AI infrastructure investment drives HBM and advanced memory demand.
