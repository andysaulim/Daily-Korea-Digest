New satellite photos confirm expansion of North Korea's Yongbyon uranium-enrichment plant

The Wall Street Journal published new satellite photographs showing expansion of the uranium-enrichment facility at Yongbyon, the same day the International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA) said North Korea has completed external works on an enrichment facility extension and voiced serious concern over the upgrades.

The IAEA finding, corroborated by Reuters, AP, and France 24, aligns with the BP Locations tracker showing a new centrifuge hall operational since April 2026 with thermal signatures consistent with increased production.

Dong-A Ilbo, ROK Politics

Democratic Party targets December 3 for Supreme Court chief justice impeachment motion

The Democratic Party of Korea formally adopted the impeachment of Supreme Court Chief Justice Cho Hee-dae as party policy on November 20, naming Rep. Yang Bu-nam to lead a task force, and SBS reported the party has set December 3, the second anniversary of the martial law declaration, as the target date to introduce the motion.

With 161 seats, the party can pass the motion without opposition support; the constitution requires introduction by at least 100 members and passage by 150. The People Power Party announced it would defend Cho and held a rally in Daegu that the party claimed drew 100,000 participants.

CNA / Yahoo, DPRK

Kim inspects educational factory sites as KCNA ignores Trump's Asia trip outreach

North Korean state media released images Saturday showing Kim Jong Un examining teaching aids and science class equipment at factory sites, with KCNA saying he inspected samples of school fixtures "of all indices."

Trump departed Asia after the APEC summit in Shenzhen without meeting Kim, saying the encounter did not "work out" but that the meeting would happen "at some point"; Pyongyang has not commented on Trump's remarks. Trump told reporters that the US-China-Russia trilateral at APEC discussed Korean Peninsula dangers.
