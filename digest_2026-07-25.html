Technology

Samsung, SK Group seal $950B in AI chip partnership deals at Lee's San Francisco summit

South Korean conglomerates and global tech giants signed $950 billion in cooperation agreements at a San Francisco summit convened by President Lee Jae-myung on July 24, with Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix among the Korean signatories partnering with US AI and technology firms. Lee simultaneously issued a 'San Francisco AI Declaration' committing Korea to becoming an 'irreplaceable, core nation in the AI supply chain,' with OpenAI CEO Sam Altman and Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang in attendance alongside Samsung Executive Chairman Lee Jae-yong and SK Group Chairman Chey Tae-won. Naver separately announced a $10 billion investment from Nvidia and Brookfield.

So what — The deals represent Korea's largest single-event commercial alignment with US tech, arriving the day after the Section 122 surcharge expired and as the USTR Section 301 investigation into Korea remains active.

38 North publishes analysis of DPRK nuclear doctrine and US-ROK alliance countermeasures

38 North published an analysis of North Korea's nuclear doctrine and its implications for strategic countermeasures available to the US-ROK alliance, arriving seven days after Kim Jong Un's July 11-12 military command meeting formally directed expansion of nuclear forces 'both in quality and quantity.' The analysis lands two days before the July 27 armistice anniversary, a date the DPRK has historically used for military displays; Kim Jong Un has not made a confirmed public appearance since July 18.

So what — Directly relevant to the July 27 armistice anniversary watch period and ongoing US-ROK extended deterrence consultations.

Pattern: On July 26, 2014, North Korea fired a Scud missile on the eve of the armistice anniversary; on July 27, 2023, Russian Defense Minister Shoigu visited Pyongyang for the 70th armistice commemoration (CSIS NK-Russia database).
