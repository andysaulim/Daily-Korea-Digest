DPRK

Seoul court orders North Korea to pay $32.6M for demolishing Kaesong liaison office

The Seoul Central District Court on Wednesday ordered North Korea to pay 44.6 billion won (approximately $32.6 million) in damages to the ROK government for the June 2020 demolition of the Inter-Korean Liaison Office in Kaesong — the first case in which Seoul has directly sued Pyongyang. NK News reports the ruling marks a legal precedent, though enforcement remains practically impossible absent DPRK assets subject to ROK jurisdiction. The demolition followed Pyongyang's decision to cut inter-Korean communication lines amid a dispute over South Korean activist groups flying anti-DPRK leaflets across the border.

NK-Russia-China

DPRK Vice Defense Minister attends Beijing Xiangshan Forum for first time in seven years

North Korea dispatched a defense ministry delegation led by Vice Defense Minister Kim Kang Il to Beijing for the 13th Xiangshan Forum — China's flagship annual security conference — the first DPRK participation since 2019. The delegation departed Pyongyang on Tuesday. The visit coincides with Kim Jong Un's Sept 15 message to Putin pledging support for Russia's 'sacred war,' placing the DPRK in simultaneous military-diplomatic engagement with both Beijing and Moscow.

ROK FM Cho Hyun to meet Secretary Rubio in Washington Friday amid investment and Hormuz discussions

Foreign Minister Cho Hyun will travel to Washington on Friday to meet Secretary of State Marco Rubio, the Foreign Ministry confirmed. The agenda covers implementation of US-ROK summit agreements, with the Hormuz Strait naval deployment question — Seoul is weighing contributing a destroyer — as a key outstanding item. The meeting follows Tuesday's US Ambassador–Vice FM talks on summit implementation, and comes as the industry ministry delayed its National Assembly briefing on the first US investment project pending further negotiation.
