US-Korea

Seoul summons Ukrainian envoy, demands apology over unilateral NK POW disclosure

South Korea's Foreign Ministry called in Ukrainian chargé d'affaires Andrii Vieshin on Monday after President Zelenskyy publicly disclosed at the UN General Assembly that two North Korean prisoners of war had been transferred to South Korea — a fact Seoul says was covered by a bilateral non-disclosure agreement. First Vice FM Park Yoon-ju demanded a formal apology and non-recurrence; Ukraine's presidential office had denied any such agreement existed. Seoul held the POWs after they were captured fighting for Russian forces in Ukraine and transferred under a confidential arrangement; the disclosure now intersects with Seoul's broader effort to avoid direct diplomatic exposure over North Korean troop deployments.

Yonhap, Bloomberg, Reuters, CNN, Washington Post, AP, Financial Times — Sep 28
DPRK

Witness statements point to wooden-box landmine at DMZ blast site; defense minister vows response

Soldiers' accounts from the September 21 DMZ explosion that injured three ROK troops describe an undetonated wooden-box landmine spotted at the blast site — a design consistent with North Korean-origin mines. ROK military and UN Command completed a first joint on-site investigation Sunday and plan further probes; Defense Minister Kang Shin-chul vowed 'appropriate corresponding measures' if the mine is confirmed as North Korean. Wooden-box mines are a known DPRK mine type deployed along the DMZ and were implicated in a 2015 blast that maimed two ROK soldiers near Paju.

Yonhap, Reuters — Sep 28
NK-Russia-China

Russia's new ambassador presents credentials in Pyongyang, formally begins posting

Andrei Podelyshev, Russia's newly arrived ambassador to North Korea, presented credentials to Kim Myong Jin, head of the DPRK Foreign Ministry's Second Protocol Department, on Monday — marking the formal start of his posting after arriving Saturday. Podelyshev replaces the previous envoy; his arrival was reported by NK News on September 26. The formal credentialing comes as the Tumangang–Khasan road bridge opened September 8 and the Sinuiju–Dandong crossing approaches completion, deepening the bilateral infrastructure underpinning the cooperation axis.
