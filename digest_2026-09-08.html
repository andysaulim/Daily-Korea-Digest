Kim Jong Un commissions Kang Kon destroyer, declares it part of nuclear response system

Russia and North Korea open first road bridge across shared Tumen River border

Russia and North Korea opened their first road bridge over the Tumen River on September 7, adding a vehicle crossing to the existing railway corridor and providing a new surface transport route for bilateral trade and military logistics. Multiple outlets including AP, BBC, Guardian, Reuters, and Al Jazeera covered the ceremony; KCNA described the bridge as a 'new symbol of friendship.' The bridge complements a rail crossing that has carried an estimated 15,800 containers across 64 voyages since September 2023.

So what — Expands the bilateral logistics corridor between Russia and North Korea at the same moment DPRK is accelerating weapons production and the NK-Russia arms transfer pipeline — a direct input to the CSIS NK-Russia cooperation timeline.
