Seoul and Washington agree to postpone Security Consultative Meeting to early 2027

South Korea and the United States agreed at the margins of the ASEAN Defence Ministers' Meeting Plus in the Philippines to hold the 58th Security Consultative Meeting (SCM) in early 2027 rather than in 2026. ROK Defense Minister Kang Shin-chul and US Secretary of Defense Pete Hegseth held their first face-to-face bilateral meeting at the ADMM-Plus gathering in Cebu. The two ministers agreed that wartime operational control (OPCON) transfer should proceed on schedule and pledged to cooperate for a smooth handover, with a separate bilateral defense ministerial set for Washington next month.

Dong-A Ilbo / Yonhap / Reuters, Technology

North Korean IPs attempt 4.4 million scans on Korean financial sector; breaches confirmed at Bithumb, securities firms

The Financial Security Institute alerted South Korean financial companies on October 21 that more than 4.4 million probe-type access attempts originating from North Korean IP ranges had been detected in a single morning.

Separately confirmed breaches hit Daishin Securities, crypto exchange Bithumb, and Mirae Asset Management, extending a wave that began earlier in October at commercial banks. The Presidential Office's National Security Council convened an emergency response meeting, calling for strengthened security and prevention of secondary damage.

WSJ, ROK Economy

Bank of Korea holds rate steady at 2.50% after back-to-back hikes

The Bank of Korea kept its base rate at 2.50% at its October meeting, pausing after consecutive hikes as policymakers weigh the KOSPI surge past 6,000 against slowing consumer spending.

Korean financial media reported divergent assessments from the central bank and securities analysts over whether the rally reflects sustainable fundamentals or speculative pressure. The November decision will be watched for signals on the rate path given export-driven growth of 1.0% in Q3 2026.
