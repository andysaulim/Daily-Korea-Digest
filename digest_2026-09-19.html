●Top Stories
DPRK

Kim Jong Un inspects munitions factories over three days, orders AI and automation upgrades to weapons production

Kim Jong Un personally inspected multiple major munitions factories from Wednesday to Friday under the Second Economic Commission, calling for expanded production capacity and modernization through artificial intelligence and automation, state media reported Saturday. Kim reviewed production at several enterprises and issued guidance for increasing output — the inspection tour runs concurrent with his 11th day of absence from confirmed public state media appearances since the Sep 8 Kang Kon destroyer commissioning. He also called for North Korean artillery to achieve the 'most concentrated and destructive' strike capability, per a separate KCNA readout via Yonhap.

NK News / Yonhap, Sep 19
DPRK

Kim Yo Jong rejects IAEA resolution, declares North Korea's nuclear status 'irreversible' amid UN agency rebuke

Kim Yo Jong issued a statement rejecting the IAEA Board of Governors resolution condemning North Korea's nuclear program, declaring the DPRK's nuclear status 'irreversible' and threatening a 'more offensive' response to US-led military drills in the Pacific. The statement, relayed via Reuters and Bloomberg, comes one week after Kim Yo Jong's prior 'absolute' nuclear status declaration and follows the DPRK vice defense minister's justification of the nuclear arsenal at the Chinese Xiangshan Defense Forum. North Korea's vice foreign minister is scheduled to attend the UN General Assembly next week, where the nuclear program is expected to feature prominently.

Yonhap / Reuters / Bloomberg, Sep 19
US-Korea

Justice Minister nominee Kim Seung-won withdraws amid lobbying allegations, fifth personnel failure under Lee government

Justice Minister nominee Kim Seung-won, a Democratic Party lawmaker, withdrew his candidacy Saturday — 20 days after nomination and two days after the National Assembly transmitted the confirmation hearing report to the Blue House. Kim cited the desire not to burden the government and said he firmed his decision after watching President Lee Jae-myung's press conference the previous day; media reported a petition from former aides alleging sexual harassment, additional drunk driving, and aide intimidation had reached the presidential office Sep 17, which Kim's office disputed. The withdrawal is the fifth personnel failure under the Lee administration, per Newsis, arriving as presidential approval hits a fresh low of 37%.

Yonhap / Reuters / Bloomberg, Sep 19
US-Korea

Cho and Rubio agree to work toward 'good results' on Korea's US investment projects; Hormuz non-combat contribution reaffirmed

South Korean Foreign Minister Cho Tae-yul and US Secretary of State Marco Rubio met in Washington and agreed to cooperate for 'good results' from ongoing talks on South Korean investment projects in the United States. Cho reaffirmed Seoul's willingness to contribute substantively to restoring free passage through the Hormuz Strait, while the Blue House separately clarified that President Lee's 'no war involvement' statement is not a rejection of Trump's request. The US Embassy Seoul confirmed Rubio stressed the importance of a fair and transparent business environment for US firms operating in Korea, and discussed the bilateral investment commitment and freedom of navigation.

September 19, 2018   ROK President Moon Jae-in and North Korean leader Kim Jong Un signed the Pyongyang Joint Declaration during Moon's summit in Pyongyang, agreeing to halt hostile acts, dismantle the Dongchang-ri missile engine test site, and conditionally close Yongbyon.
