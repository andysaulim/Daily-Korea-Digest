●Top Stories
DPRK

North Korea fires multiple short-range ballistic missiles toward East Sea from Wonsan

North Korea launched several short-range ballistic missiles from near Wonsan starting at 5:20 a.m. KST Saturday, flying approximately 250 km before landing in the East Sea, according to the ROK Joint Chiefs of Staff. USFK and INDOPACOM confirmed awareness of the launch and said they assessed no immediate threat, reaffirming alliance defense commitments. The AP reported the launch followed a joint ROK-US military drill that Pyongyang characterized as a provocation — the CSIS database records an identical SRBM volley from Wonsan on Sep 12, 2024.

US-Korea

Approximately 2,000 march in Seoul against Hormuz Strait deployment; rallies held nationwide

Civil society groups including Chamyeo Yondae and Jaju Tongil Pyeonghwa Yondae assembled at Jongno, Seoul on Saturday afternoon, marching to the presidential residence chanting 'Refuse Hormuz deployment — do not cooperate with a war of aggression.' Anti-deployment rallies also convened in Daejeon, with organizers framing troop dispatch as entry into a Trump-initiated war. The demonstration follows Foreign Minister Cho Hyun's statement to Iran on Sep 11 that no deployment decision has been made, and the return of an ROK assessment team from the UAE on Sep 10.

Japan-Korea

Japan holds Sado mine memorial without acknowledging forced labor for third consecutive year; South Korea boycotts for third year

Japan's government representative at the Sep 12 Sado island gold mine memorial — Foreign Ministry official Hamamoto Yukiya — stated that Korean Peninsula workers 'labored under dangerous and harsh conditions,' using language virtually identical to prior years, without acknowledging the coercive nature of the labor. South Korea's government boycotted for the third consecutive year, having failed to reach agreement with Tokyo on the wording of the memorial's acknowledgment. Japan registered Sado as a UNESCO World Cultural Heritage site in 2024 with a commitment to hold annual memorial events.
