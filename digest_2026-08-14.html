N. Korea warns of 'new level of deterrent' ahead of Ulchi Freedom Shield

North Korea on Friday denounced the upcoming US-ROK Ulchi Freedom Shield exercise and vowed to field a 'new level of deterrent,' per KCNA reporting relayed by Yonhap and Reuters. The warning is DPRK's second major exercise-related statement this week, following the Aug 13 'unsurvivable retaliation' threat over ROK's nuclear submarine program. Kim Jong Un has not appeared publicly in 17 days, with Liberation Day (Aug 15) and the exercise start (Aug 17) both imminent.

So what — Directly affects the Ulchi Freedom Shield opening Aug 17 and the Liberation Day address by President Lee on Aug 15.

NK Pro: North Korea is China's largest overseas wine market for fourth consecutive year
North Korea imported 442,311 liters of Chinese wine in the first half of 2026, making it China's largest foreign wine market by volume for the fourth year running — ranking second only to Hong Kong among all territories, per NK Pro analysis of China General Administration of Customs data.
