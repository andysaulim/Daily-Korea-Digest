●Top Stories
DPRK

North Korea fires two short-range ballistic missiles from Wonsan toward the East Sea within three hours

Kim Jong Un's military launched one SRBM at approximately 15:00 KST that flew about 450 km, then fired a second at 17:50 that flew more than 600 km — an unusual staggered-launch pattern that ROK JCS said is under joint ROK-US precision analysis. The launches are the 14th and 15th ballistic missile events of 2026 and come eight days after the September 12 multi-type firepower drill, three days after Kim Yo Jong's third consecutive day of escalatory statements, and four days before a reported US-China summit — with 동아일보 and 경향신문 citing analyst assessments that Pyongyang is sending a non-denuclearization signal ahead of Trump-Xi talks. Japan's defense minister condemned the launches as a 'grave threat' to regional peace and said Tokyo protested through Beijing diplomatic channels; both missiles fell outside Japan's exclusive economic zone.

NK-Russia-China

Heavy truck spotted on unopened Yalu River bridge, stoking rumors of imminent opening of Sinuiju–Dandong crossing

Yonhap's Beijing bureau reported Sunday that a heavy truck was observed crossing the new Yalu River bridge connecting Sinuiju (DPRK) and Dandong (China) — the new crossing that 38 North satellite imagery assessed on September 11 as appearing close to opening. The sighting follows the September 8 inauguration of the Tumen River road bridge linking North Korea and Russia, and NBC News analysis from September 15 framed both crossings as simultaneous infrastructure bets deepening Pyongyang's connectivity with its two patrons. Regular opening of the new Sinuiju–Dandong bridge would provide a second road artery complementing the existing 1943-era Friendship Bridge, which handles the bulk of DPRK-China overland trade.

Yonhap EN (Beijing bureau)
Trilateral

Korea, US, and Japan to sign Arctic economic security MOU at UNGA on September 22

ROK FM Cho Hyun, US SecState Marco Rubio, and Japan's foreign minister are expected to sign a Memorandum of Understanding on Arctic economic security cooperation in New York on September 22 at the margins of the UN General Assembly, per Japan's Yomiuri Shimbun citing the plans. The MOU would establish a working group — provisionally named the 'Arctic-Pacific Trilateral Partnership' — to expand Arctic energy production and supply, share icebreaker technology, and counter Russian Arctic military expansion and Chinese resource extraction. The timing is notable: DPRK Vice FM Kim Son-gyong is also expected at UNGA the same week, and the North fired two SRBMs earlier the same day.
