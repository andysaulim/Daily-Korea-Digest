Korea Times, ROK Economy

South Korea Q3 GDP grows 1.0%, triple Bank of Korea forecast, on AI chip surge

South Korea's real GDP rose 1.0% quarter-on-quarter in the July-September period, tripling the Bank of Korea's own 0.3% projection, driven by booming semiconductor exports, the central bank said Tuesday.

Growth rebounded to 1.8% in Q1, and with three consecutive above-forecast quarters, some analysts now see annual growth reaching 4%. The Bank of Korea separately warned that chip exports could fall by up to $100 billion if AI spending slows.

NK News / Korea Times, DPRK

North Korea forward-deploys 122mm self-propelled artillery along western border, visible from Gimpo

NK News photography from the Aegibong observatory in Gimpo captured at least three North Korean 122mm self-propelled howitzers deployed along the Jogang River coastal border area of Kaepung County, Hwanghae Province, farther south than their usual positions. South Korea's Joint Chiefs of Staff assessed the weapons as legacy systems, not the new artillery Pyongyang recently threatened to forward-deploy, and said the deployment was "in plain view."

Defense Minister Kang Shin-chul's separate remark, "Can we really trust the US military?", at a National Assembly hearing prompted a ministry clarification that the comment was meant to argue for wartime operational control (OPCON) transfer, not to question alliance reliability.

NK News, DPRK

Swiss foreign minister to visit Pyongyang Oct 29-30 to open first dedicated Swiss political office

Swiss Foreign Minister Ignazio Cassis will make a two-day visit to North Korea beginning October 29, attending the official opening of the first dedicated Swiss political office in Pyongyang and meeting DPRK officials, according to the Swiss government.

The visit is the highest-level Western diplomatic trip to Pyongyang in years. Switzerland has served as a protecting power for the United States in North Korea.
