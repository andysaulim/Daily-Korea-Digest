Kim Jong Un and Putin exchange Liberation Day messages reaffirming ties; Putin declares security coordination

Kim Jong Un sent Putin a message on Liberation Day expecting a 'more excellent future' in bilateral ties, while Putin separately stated via KCNA that Russia and North Korea are 'actively cooperating in all sectors' and 'coordinating on regional security.' NK, China, Russia state-sponsored cyberattacks rise 7.5% in H1 2026; NK most active actor
Cybersecurity firm S2W reported that state-sponsored APT incidents from North Korea, China, and Russia rose 7.5% globally in the first half of 2026 compared to the preceding six months, with the escalation concentrated in Q1.
