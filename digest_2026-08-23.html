ROK military: No signs of imminent additional North Korean troop deployment to Russia

South Korea's military confirmed Sunday it has detected no indications of an imminent additional dispatch of North Korean troops to Russia, the first official ROK assessment since Kim Yo Jong issued coordinated public denials on Aug 20-21. The statement does not address the status or rotation of the estimated tens of thousands of troops already deployed. The ROK assessment aligns with Kim Yo Jong's denial but leaves open whether a rotation or incremental expansion below detection thresholds is underway.

So what — Directly relevant to the ongoing CSIS NK-Russia cooperation timeline and the September 9 DPRK founding day watch window.

Pattern: Kim Yo Jong issued coordinated dual denials of US-DPRK contact and additional Russia troop deployments on Aug 20-21, per the DPRK official statements history tracker.

38 North: North Korea's restrained signaling toward China continues

38 North published analysis finding that North Korea has maintained a restrained signaling posture toward China, a pattern that persists despite the recent SRBM salvo during Ulchi Freedom Shield and Kim Yo Jong's coordinated denials of US-DPRK contact. Business

Economists expect BOK to raise 2026 growth forecast above 3%

Economists surveyed by Yonhap expect the Bank of Korea to upgrade its 2026 GDP growth forecast to above 3% at its next scheduled update, compared with the current OECD estimate of 2.6% and the BOK's own prior forecast. The anticipated revision is attributed to stronger-than-expected export recovery. The BOK rate currently stands at 2.50% following a change in August 2026.

So what — A BOK growth upgrade above 3% would be the first since before the Yoon impeachment period and would affect fiscal planning and National Assembly budget deliberations.
