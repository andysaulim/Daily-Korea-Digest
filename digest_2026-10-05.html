●Top Stories
DPRK

ROK military confirms NK mines planted south of MDL caused DMZ blast; launches removal operation, demands apology

South Korea's Joint Chiefs of Staff, in a joint investigation with UN Command, concluded that plastic North Korean anti-personnel mines deliberately buried south of the Military Demarcation Line caused the September 21 DMZ blast injuring three ROK soldiers. The JCS assessed the mines were installed more than 12 months ago during DPRK border fortification operations and launched a mine-removal operation at the site this afternoon. The ruling Democratic Party and the People Power Party both called on North Korea to immediately apologize and remove remaining mines, while the JCS said inter-Korean military talks are 'absolutely necessary.'

DPRK

Kim Jong Un oversees IRBM hypersonic missile drill; Kim Yo Jong publicly claims AI-guided evasion for first time

KCNA confirmed Kim Jong Un personally oversaw an intermediate-range hypersonic missile firing drill toward the East Sea, with his daughter Ju Ae present, and vowed to rapidly advance North Korea's 'nuclear war deterrent.' Kim Yo Jong separately stated the test involved AI-guided low-altitude maneuvering — the first time Pyongyang has publicly attributed AI guidance to a strategic missile — and KCNA mocked Seoul and Tokyo for allegedly failing to track the missile. The drill occurred five days before the October 10 Workers' Party founding anniversary, when Punggye-ri remains assessed at test-ready status.
