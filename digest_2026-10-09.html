●Top Stories
US-Korea

South Korea test-flies first domestically developed hypersonic glide vehicle

South Korea successfully test-launched its first domestically developed hypersonic glide vehicle on October 8, with President Lee Jae-myung observing the launch. The Blue House said the test marked a significant step toward securing a new strategic deterrent capability; the HGV travels at more than Mach 5 and is designed to evade air-defense interception. The test comes weeks after North Korea conducted its own hypersonic missile test, and the same day South Korea's MND noted ongoing DPRK landmine incidents on the DMZ.

Washington Post / AP / NK News, Oct 8-9
US-Korea

Seoul recalls Ukraine ambassador over North Korean POW transfer dispute, triggering domestic backlash

South Korea recalled Ambassador Park Ki-chang from Kyiv after Ukraine transferred captured North Korean soldiers to Seoul without coordinating in a manner that met South Korea's expectations. Ukraine said it remains open to dialogue; the People Power Party accused the Lee government of picking a fight with Ukraine while remaining 'biasedly silent' toward North Korea. The recall directly affects South Korea's ability to receive future North Korean POW transfers, which require Ukrainian cooperation.

NK News / Reuters / AP / NYT, Oct 9
Technology

AI-assisted cyberattacks buffet South Korean and Japanese financial institutions; police expand probe

South Korean police expanded their hacking investigation team after 16 of 18 suspected overseas hacking attacks on South Korean financial firms went unattributed, according to a lawmaker's disclosure. CrowdStrike identified a Chinese-developed AI agent called ARTEX as a tool used in attacks on South Korean banks; its developer made the tool closed-source following the disclosure. Reuters reported AI is lowering the barrier for cybercriminals targeting South Korea and Japan, with bank-sector intrusions sweeping both countries.
