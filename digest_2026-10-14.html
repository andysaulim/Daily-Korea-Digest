DPRK

North Korea Opens Defense Revolution Museum Featuring Hwasong-20 ICBM as Trump Floats Kim Meeting

Kim Jong Un opened the National Defense Revolution Museum on October 13, showcasing the Hwasong-20 ICBM and other strategic weapons capable of reaching the continental United States, vowing to 'more vigorously develop Juche weapons.' President Donald Trump simultaneously said he 'may' meet Kim during his upcoming November Asia trip. NK Pro analysis found the museum contains over 25 missile launch vehicles and 15 types of drones and naval weapons, and may be located at a suspected nuclear site.

DOJ Charges 14, Seizes 187 Devices in Crackdown on North Korean IT Worker Schemes at 70+ US Firms

The US Department of Justice charged 14 people, arrested two, and seized 187 computer devices in a crackdown on North Korean remote IT worker schemes that penetrated over 70 American companies. The operation targeted individuals who helped DPRK workers obtain fraudulent employment and funnel revenue to Pyongyang. A laptop farm operation in Texas was among the targets.

China-Korea

New Yalu River Bridge Linking China and North Korea to Open Thursday, More Than a Decade Late

China and North Korea will officially open the New Yalu River Bridge connecting Dandong and Sinuiju on Thursday, October 15, according to Xinhua — more than a decade after the span was constructed. The bridge completion signals a new phase of NK-China cross-border economic connectivity, arriving as NK-Russia road infrastructure (Tumangang–Khasan bridge, opened Sep 8, 2026) also became operational.

US-Korea

DOJ Drops Charges Against Former CIA Analyst Sue Mi Terry in South Korea Intelligence Case

US federal prosecutors agreed to drop charges against Sue Mi Terry, a former CIA analyst and prominent North Korea expert accused of secretly working for South Korea's intelligence agency. The case, which sent shockwaves through the Korea policy community when announced approximately two years ago, was headed for trial when the agreement was reached. Terry had faced charges of acting as an unregistered agent of a foreign government.
