Dong-A Ilbo, NK-Russia-China

North Korean envoy's Beijing visit fuels Kim-Xi summit speculation ahead of APEC

Kim Song Nam, the Workers' Party international secretary and Pyongyang's top liaison to Beijing, returned home November 7 after a five-day China visit during which he met Politburo Standing Committee member Cai Qi and Foreign Minister Wang Yi. The visit came ten days before US President Donald Trump is due to attend the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation summit in Shenzhen on November 18, and followed Kim Jong Un's unannounced day trip to Dandong on October 24. Analysts cited in Dong-A Ilbo suggest Kim Song Nam may have relayed messages toward Washington through Beijing, while the back-to-back high-level contacts have fueled speculation about a preparatory visit by Kim Jong Un and a summit with Xi.

NK News, NK-Russia-China

Zelensky says Russia used North Korean KN-23 missiles to strike Ukrainian energy facility

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky said Saturday that Russia used North Korean KN-23 short-range ballistic missiles to damage a key Ukrainian energy facility, calling it proof that international sanctions must be strengthened.

Zelensky said preliminary findings confirmed KN-23 use, naming the model for the first time in public attribution, previous statements had referred generically to 'North Korean ballistic missiles.' Ukraine's military is separately investigating the strike.

Reuters, ROK Economy

South Korea fines Coupang $220 million for abusing dominance in food delivery market

South Korea's Fair Trade Commission fined Coupang $220 million for abusing its dominant position in the food delivery market, regulators confirmed. SBS reported separately that the total combined fines against Coupang Eats and Baemin reached approximately 630 billion won ($463 million) for pressuring merchants to sell at lower prices, limiting price competition and raising costs for consumers.
