North Korea threatens South Korea with 'collective punishment' over US defence hub role; rejects nuclear test halt call

A North Korean official declared South Korea would become a target of 'collective punishment' if Seoul transforms into a hub for US collective defense, per a Yonhap/Reuters report citing KCNA. US-Korea

President Lee accuses Zelensky of violating non-disclosure agreement by publicly announcing North Korean POW transfer to South Korea

Lee Jae-myung posted on X that Ukraine's president violated a confidentiality agreement when he publicly announced at UNGA on September 23 that two North Korean prisoners of war had been transferred to South Korea. A Blue House official clarified Seoul had received vague prior diplomatic notice but that the exact form of disclosure was not specified; the official stated the government had consistently sought to handle the matter quietly for safety and humanitarian reasons. The ruling Democratic Party called it a breach of bilateral trust, while the opposition People Power Party accused the government of being 'only mindful of Kim Jong-un' for keeping the transfer secret from the public.

JCS chief visits 25th Division on Chuseok to inspect DMZ landmine blast probe; DPRK mine emplacement not ruled out

JCS Chairman Gen. Jin Yong-seop traveled to the 25th Division on Chuseok to review the Joint Field Investigation Task Force's preparedness for an on-site probe into the September 21 DMZ blast that injured three soldiers including a battalion commander. The JCS rebutted claims that injured personnel were evacuated by US military helicopters, stating ROK Army Medevac helicopters (KUH-1M) performed the airlift. The Blue House confirmed that North Korean mine emplacement remains one of the hypotheses under investigation, alongside the possibility of legacy mines from the area where North Korea conducted a box mine provocation in 2015.
