Yangsan records 42.5°C — Korea's highest temperature since modern observation began in 1904

Yangsan, South Gyeongsang Province recorded 42.5°C at 1:26 p.m. on Sunday, surpassing all prior readings from the KMA's 97 long-term observation stations dating to 1904. Sunday marked the fifth consecutive day temperatures exceeded 40°C in southeastern Korea, with 40°C-plus readings also reported in northern Busan, Gimhae, Gyeongju, and Gwangyang. The Korea Meteorological Administration upgraded heat wave advisories to warnings across all of Seoul by 11 a.m.

So what — Sustained extreme heat is driving a spike in household electricity demand and credit loans, with direct implications for energy policy, the Korea Electric Power Corp's grid stability, and BOK consumer credit monitoring.

Korea Times / Yonhap English / Bloomberg / CNA, Aug 2, 2026

ROKS Daejeon wins RIMPAC top gun honors — first ROK warship in 16 years
The ROK Navy's 3,100-ton frigate ROKS Daejeon scored the highest points among 13 warships from 10 nations in the Naval Surface Fire Support Rodeo competition held in Hawaii during RIMPAC, the Navy announced Sunday. It is the first time a Korean warship has won the competition since the destroyer King Sejong the Great took the prize in 2010. Korea served as commander of the Combined Force during this year's RIMPAC, underscoring an elevated ROK role in multilateral maritime exercises as US-ROK-Japan trilateral naval cooperation deepens.
