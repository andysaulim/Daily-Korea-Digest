●Top Stories
DPRK

Kim Jong Un oversees IRBM hypersonic drill; KCNA claims AI-guided missile flew 1,000 km, mocking Seoul and Tokyo's tracking failure

Kim Jong Un personally oversaw a strategic missile firing drill toward the East Sea in the early morning of October 3, with KCNA reporting the intermediate-range missile struck a designated target approximately 1,000 kilometers away. Kim Yo Jong confirmed the drill via statement, claiming the weapon's low-altitude maneuvering flight — described as AI-guided — prevented South Korean and Japanese militaries from tracking its full trajectory. ROK MND issued an immediate counter-statement asserting that the missile was monitored from the preparation stage and is fully within the detection and interception capability of combined ROK-US defense assets.

NK News · Reuters · Yonhap · Bloomberg · WaPo · AP · Oct 4
Technology

South Korean president orders probe into AI-driven hacking wave hitting seven financial institutions

A coordinated series of AI-assisted cyberattacks struck at least seven South Korean financial institutions — four banks, two savings banks, and one capital company — with attackers using the AI agent 'Artex' to simultaneously probe back-door vulnerabilities rather than traditional malware-based server takeovers. Attack servers were found to contain Chinese-language strings, though attribution has not been formally established. President Lee Jae-myung ordered a thorough investigation and the Financial Supervisory Service convened an emergency meeting with financial firm heads; the Ministry of Science and ICT activated a 24-hour emergency response system and recommended security checks for approximately 28,000 companies.

Reuters · Yonhap · 동아일보 · 경향신문 · 한국경제 · Oct 4
US-Korea

President Lee invokes October 4 inter-Korean summit declaration spirit on 19th anniversary, calling for return to dialogue

Lee Jae-myung issued a statement on October 4 marking the 19th anniversary of the October 4 South-North Joint Declaration (2007 Roh-Kim summit), calling for a return to the spirit of that accord. The statement comes one day after North Korea conducted an IRBM drill and amid continued Kim Yo Jong escalatory DMZ statements, placing Lee's inter-Korean engagement posture in direct contrast with Pyongyang's current trajectory.
