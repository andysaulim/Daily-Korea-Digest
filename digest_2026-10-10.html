US expects South Korea to 'uphold alliance standards' amid intelligence cooperation concerns

A senior US official said Friday that Washington expects Seoul to 'uphold alliance standards' after concerns surfaced about South Korea's intelligence and counterintelligence management — the sharpest public US statement about the bilateral intelligence relationship since the Lee government took office in June 2025. The statement follows a series of friction points including the visa delay affecting a senior ROK lawmaker and the spy agency snub reported by Yonhap on October 9. Seoul has not yet issued a formal response to the US official's remarks.

US-Korea

South Korean ambassador to Ukraine returns home amid dispute over North Korean POW disclosure

South Korea's ambassador to Ukraine Park Ki-chang returned to Seoul on Saturday morning, two days after his formal recall was announced — the first such South Korean diplomatic recall in 18 years according to Hankyoreh reporting. The dispute centers on Kyiv's public disclosure of the identities of two captured North Korean soldiers, which Seoul says violated an intelligence-sharing agreement and could endanger the soldiers and their families. Ukraine's foreign ministry said 'diplomacy must prevail over emotion' and opposition PPP lawmaker Na Kyung-won sent a letter of thanks to President Zelensky, drawing a sharp rebuke from the ruling Democratic Party as 'treasonous.'
