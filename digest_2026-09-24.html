●Top Stories
DPRK

DPRK FM Choe Son Hui rejects US-ROK-Japan denuclearization call as 'unrealistic,' vows to keep nuclear weapons 'forever'

North Korean Foreign Minister Choe Son Hui issued a statement through KCNA on Thursday rejecting the joint US-ROK-Japan call for Pyongyang to denuclearize and return to dialogue, declaring the country's will to retain nuclear weapons is 'unchanged' and 'irreversible through all ages.' The statement came the same day Russia formally invited Kim Jong Un to Moscow and one day after Trump reaffirmed commitment to dialogue with North Korea in his meeting with President Lee. Choe's statement preempts any diplomatic opening the US-China summit this week might generate for Korean Peninsula diplomacy.

NK-Russia-China

Ukraine transfers two North Korean POWs to South Korea; Zelensky announces at UNGA

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky announced Wednesday that Ukraine had transferred two North Korean soldiers — captured during its incursion into Russia's Kursk region in early 2025 — to South Korea. Russia stated it had no information on the transfer, while SBS reported Seoul may have provided weapons aid to Ukraine in exchange. The two POWs are the first confirmed DPRK soldiers transferred to Seoul, providing direct intelligence on DPRK troop deployment, training, and order-of-battle in Russia.

DPRK

North Korea tests upgraded 240mm MLRS, claims extended range; weapons to be deployed to South Korean border

KCNA reported Thursday that North Korea successfully tested an upgraded 240mm multiple-launch rocket system (MLRS) on Tuesday, with top military officials overseeing the test. State media said the weapons will be deployed to the South Korean border 'to constantly expose the enemy to extreme uneasiness and fear.' The test follows the Sep 21 hypersonic-linked weapons test overseen by Kim Jong Un, constituting two kinetic demonstrations in three days.
