●Top Stories
US-Korea

Lee and Trump hold ~30-minute UNGA bilateral; reaffirm NK dialogue, OPCON transfer, and $350B investment progress

Presidents Lee Jae-myung and Donald Trump met on UNGA sidelines in New York on Sep 22, with ROK NSA Wi Seong-rak confirming the two leaders welcomed 'significant progress' on bilateral strategic investment projects and reaffirmed Trump's commitment to US-North Korea dialogue. The Hankyoreh separately reports this was the first meeting in which OPCON transfer was formally raised — though the Hormuz deployment issue was not discussed — and that Lee's UNGA address was revised at the last moment to substitute 'a Korean Peninsula without nuclear weapons' for 'denuclearization.' The meeting followed designation of a Texas gas plant as the first US strategic investment project, which Reuters and FT report was the enabling condition for the summit to occur.

Yonhap / Reuters / Bloomberg / 한겨레 (Sep 22-23)
DPRK

Two consecutive DMZ explosions confirmed; forensics underway as origin — DPRK mine or ROK — undetermined

The ROK Joint Chiefs confirmed two sequential blasts struck a trail-clearing patrol of 18 personnel in the western DMZ on Sep 21, with the site located tens of meters from a zone south of the MDL already suspected of DPRK mine-laying. The National Forensic Service received fragment samples and UN Command will conduct a joint field investigation; a UNC inspection of the suspected DPRK mine zone had already been scheduled for next week. Defense Minister Kang Shin-chul visited injured troops at the ROK Army Trauma Center and the 25th Infantry Division, urging troops to maintain operational discipline regardless of the outcome.

Yonhap EN / 한겨레 (Sep 23)
DPRK

Lee's UNGA address lays out three-part inter-Korean 'peaceful coexistence' vision and calls for Korean War peace regime

President Lee's speech at the 81st UN General Assembly called for mutual respect, restored communication channels, and broader inter-Korean cooperation as pillars of a proposed 'peace regime (평화체제),' pledging ROK as 'peacemaker and pacemaker' for resumed US-DPRK dialogue. NK News reports Lee outlined the vision publicly in the same week that DPRK Vice FM Kim Son-gyong departed for New York, setting up a diplomatic counterpoint at UNGA. The Hankyoreh reports Lee personally changed the final text from 'denuclearization' to 'a Korean Peninsula without nuclear weapons' — a substantive framing shift that widens the gap with the standing US-ROK-Japan joint denuclearization position reaffirmed by the three FMs on Sep 22.
