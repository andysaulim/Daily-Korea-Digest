North Korea tests new air-defense missile, conducts cruise missile airport-strike drill

Kim Jong Un's Missile Administration tested a new surface-to-air missile system on Wednesday, confirming its "intelligent multiple guided mode" and target-identification capability, while separately staging a long-range strategic cruise missile strike drill simulating an attack on an enemy airport using cluster munitions on a runway. NK News found that images published by KCNA were heavily doctored.
