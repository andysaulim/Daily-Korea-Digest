South Korea's September Exports Hit Record $120.9B as Chip Boom Rolls On

South Korea's September exports surged 83.5 percent year-on-year to a record $120.9 billion, driven by semiconductor sales, marking the first time monthly exports have exceeded $120 billion. Trump Touts $200B in South Korean Energy Investments; Seoul Disputes Alaska LNG Commitment

President Trump unveiled what he described as $200 billion in South Korean investments in US energy projects — including Alaska LNG participation, a Texas power plant, and construction of 8 nuclear reactors in the United States. Seoul's government disputed the Alaska LNG characterization, saying it was not a firm commitment, while South Korea and the United States formally selected a Texas power plant as the first project under the strategic investment framework. The US-Korea Strategic Investment Corporation, established under the Special Investment Act passed March 12 2026, is the implementation vehicle.

Lee Calls DMZ Mine Blast an 'Unfortunate Accident,' Reaffirms Dialogue Push Despite Armistice Finding

President Lee Jae-myung called the September 21 DMZ mine blast an 'unfortunate accident' on Armed Forces Day and reaffirmed his commitment to restore dialogue with North Korea, even after Seoul's military and the UN Command confirmed the explosion violated the armistice agreement. Kim Yo Jong denied DPRK responsibility, calling Seoul's account a 'self-staged farce.' Nikkei reports Lee is balancing a response to the blast with his broader dialogue posture.
