Seoul court acquits ex-President Yoon in power abuse case over ambassador appointment

A Seoul court acquitted former President Yoon Suk-yeol Friday in the power abuse case stemming from the appointment of a former envoy to Australia, a rare legal reprieve for the impeached former leader. The acquittal does not affect Yoon's ongoing appellate trial on separate charges related to free opinion polls, in which prosecutors sought a four-year prison term on the same day. Yoon was also separately acquitted in a case related to a South Korean marine's drowning.

Defense Minister nominee Kang Shin-chul told the National Assembly that South Korea will pursue retaking wartime operational control from the United States without attaching additional preconditions, aligning with outgoing Minister Ahn Kyu-baek's position that Seoul can defend itself independently. 38 North: New North Korea-China border crossing near the new Yalu River Bridge appears close to opening

Satellite imagery analyzed by 38 North shows a new North Korea-China road crossing linked to the new Yalu River Bridge at Sinuiju-Dandong appears close to opening. The development follows the March 2026 resumption of Sinuiju-Dandong passenger rail and comes days after Russia's Sep 8 opening of the first road bridge over the Tumen River connecting DPRK and Russia.
