North Korea fires ~10 short-range ballistic missiles from Wonsan, days after Seoul dialogue offer

North Korea launched a salvo of approximately 10 short-range ballistic missiles (SRBMs) from the Wonsan area toward the East Sea at around 12:30 p.m. Korea Standard Time on October 29, the ROK Joint Chiefs of Staff announced.

The missiles flew approximately 250 km, with the US and South Korea conducting joint precision analysis. The launches came days after Defense Minister Kang Shin-chul publicly called on Pyongyang to return to dialogue at the ASEAN Defence Ministers' Meeting Plus (ADMM-Plus) in the Philippines.

Korea Times / Yonhap, NK-Russia-China

NIS: Kim Jong Un may have used Dandong visit to convey Pyongyang's position on US talks

South Korea's National Intelligence Service told lawmakers in a closed-door intelligence committee audit that Kim Jong Un may have conveyed Pyongyang's stance on potential talks with the United States during his October 24 visit to Dandong, China.

The NIS noted Kim was accompanied by senior diplomats including Foreign Minister Choe Son-hui rather than military officials, suggesting a purpose beyond commemorating China's Korean War anniversary participation. The NIS added that China could relay Kim's position to Washington but does not appear to have the will to act as a broker.

Washington Post / AP, ROK Politics

Prosecutors seek death penalty for former President Yoon Suk Yeol over December martial law

South Korean prosecutors have requested the death penalty for former President Yoon Suk Yeol in connection with his December 3, 2025 declaration of martial law. The prosecution's demand represents the most severe penalty sought in the case against Yoon, who was impeached and removed from office following the failed declaration. The trial remains ongoing in Seoul.
