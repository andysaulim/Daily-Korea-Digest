Kim Yo Jong declares DPRK nuclear status 'absolute,' rejects IAEA denuclearization calls

Russia employing North Korean workers to manufacture drones under fraudulent visas

Bloomberg reported that Russia is using North Korean workers to produce drones under fake visas, a new layer of the labor-weapons nexus documented since 2023. A separate Yonhap report cited assessments putting total North Korean workers deployed abroad at up to 100,000, with some confirmed involved in Russian drone production.
